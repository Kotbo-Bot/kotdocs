<p class="doc-lead">The team does not need to open the dashboard for everyday gestures: declaring an absence, confirming attendance at a meeting or reading an evaluation all happen on Discord.</p>

:::tip[On the dashboard]
The schedule, the meetings, the directory and the evaluations are driven from the dashboard's **Team** section: [Schedule](/en/dashboard/staff/planning/) (meetings included), [Staff members](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/).
:::

:::caution[Subcommands keep their French names]
None of the commands below is localised: they are typed the same in both languages.
:::

---

## Meetings

* `/meeting create`: Schedules a staff meeting, with its title, date and description.
* `/meeting list`: The upcoming meetings.
* `/meeting checkin`: Confirms your attendance at the meeting in progress by hand.
* `/meeting sync`: Forces a sync of the absences declared for the next meeting.

## Absences and departures

  command="/absent declarer type:Congés debut:12/07/2026 fin:19/07/2026 superieur:@Alex"

`/absent declarer` opens the declaration: the **type** of absence, its **debut** and **fin** dates, the **raison** and the **superieur** to notify. `/absent terminer` closes it early.

`/demission` submits a staff resignation request with a reason. The request goes to the team: it removes no role on its own.

---

## Evaluations

* `/evaluations me`: Your own evaluation.
* `/evaluations check`: A staff member's evaluation.
* `/evaluations generate`: Generates an evaluation over a given **periode**.

---

## Training your moderators

`/simulation` starts a **simulated incident** for the team to practise on, from a chosen scenario. Nothing that happens there is real: this is the tool to pull out before handing real sanctions to a new moderator.

---

## Server administration

These commands require the **Administrator** permission.

* `/setup`: The bot's step-by-step setup assistant.
* `/config`: Configures Kotbo's features from Discord, without the dashboard.
* `/audit`: Reviews the server's security and suggests improvements.
* `/backup`: Backs the server up - roles, channels, permissions, emojis, stickers - and restores it: `create`, `list`, `restore`, `delete`, `export`, `import`. See [Backups](/en/dashboard/configuration/sauvegardes/).
* `/leveling`: Adjusts members' XP and levels.
* `/prestige-admin`: Sets up the prestige ranking.
* `/link`: Links channels together. See [Channel links](/en/discord/communaute/liens-salons/).
* `/protection`: Captcha, raid mode, locks and lockdown. See [Anti-raid protection](/en/dashboard/moderation/anti-raid/).
* `/channelhealth`: Channel analysis and archiving. See [Channel health](/en/dashboard/configuration/sante-salons/).
* `/activate`: Activates the bot on the server with an activation code.
* `/staffserver`: Links a staff server to the main one and drives the sync: `setup`, `mode`, `sync`, `mapping`, `unmap`, `remove`. See [Staff servers](/en/dashboard/cross-serveur/serveurs-staff/).
* `/status`: Checks the HTTP status of a URL.

:::note[The dashboard stays the reference]
These commands help out when nobody has the dashboard at hand. For anything set finely - per-role permissions, sanction scales, modules - the dashboard shows the whole picture, where a command only shows one field at a time.
:::
