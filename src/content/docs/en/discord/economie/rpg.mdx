## Your character's resources

* **Coins**: Buying, crafting, forging, enchanting, switching class.
* **XP**: Levelling up. Every level grants **3 stat points** and **1 skill point**.
* **HP**: Running out of health blocks fighting until you rest or drink a potion.
* **Energy**: Regenerates over time. A fight costs **15** energy, an arena duel **20**, a fishing trip **5**.

The server sets the maximum energy, the recovery rate and the payouts itself: these values differ from one server to the next.

## Character, class and skills

The **Character** tab holds the sheet: base stats (ATQ, DÉF, VIT, PV - attack, defence, speed, health), class passive, skills and points left to spend. The **+1** button under each stat spends one point.

### Picking a class

The class is chosen at **level 5** and decides your stats, your passive and your combat skills.

* **Guerrier** (Warrior): passive *Peau de fer*, cutting all damage taken by 15%. Skills: *Frappe Brutale*, a hit at 200% damage, and *Cri de Guerre*, which doubles defence for a turn and restores 10% of HP.
* **Rôdeur** (Ranger): passive *Œil de lynx*, adding 12% critical chance. Skills: *Tir Précis*, which ignores the enemy's defence, and *Esquive*, which dodges the next attack entirely.
* **Mage**: passive *Percée arcanique*, whose attacks ignore 30% of the enemy's defence. Skills: *Boule de Feu*, at 230% damage, and *Drain de Vie*, at 150% damage with half returned as HP.

:::caution[Switching class has a price]
Reclassing costs **2,500 coins**, and the skill tree resets: branches belong to a class. The points you had invested are handed back.
:::

### The skill tree

It opens along with the class and holds **three branches** per class - *Fureur*, *Rempart* and *Vétéran* for the Warrior, *Précision*, *Ombre* and *Traqueur* for the Ranger, *Arcane*, *Sang* and *Protection* for the Mage.

Every node has a rank, a point cost and sometimes a prerequisite. Kotbo says why a node stays closed: *level N required*, *requires: …*, *N point(s) required*. **Reset everything** hands the invested points back for **1,500 coins**.

## Fighting

The **Fight** button draws a monster within your reach, and the duel plays out turn by turn with four buttons: **Attack**, **Defend**, **Potion** and **Flee**. Class skills appear on top of those once off cooldown.

A win pays XP, coins, sometimes loot, and fills in the **bestiary**. A loss only costs you HP.

:::note[What stops a fight]
Less than 15 energy, HP too low, or the delay between two fights. The bot says which one every time, with the time left.
:::

The **Boss** button targets a specific creature among those the server marked as bosses. The **Bestiary** keeps the hunting log: creatures discovered out of the total, wins, losses, success rate, and a *already fought* / *never met* filter.

### The arena

Player duels, **asynchronous**: your opponent does not need to be online, their character defends on its own.

* Opens at **level 5**, **20 energy** a duel, and five minutes between two entries.
* Everyone starts at **1,000 points** of Elo rating, and the arena only offers opponents of a comparable level.
* The sheet shows your record, the streak in progress, the server ranking and the **duels you recently took** - you see who attacked you, and what it cost or earned you.

## Gear and items

The **Inventory** tab lists the bag and is where you **equip** a weapon, an armour or an accessory, or **drink** a potion. An equipped item is tagged as such.

**Accessories** take three slots, unlocked at **levels 1, 12 and 24**.

The rest of the gear is obtained and improved in the hub's workshops:

* **Shop**: Buys what the server put up for sale: weapons, armour, potions, quest items.
* **Crafting workshop**: Turns combat loot into gear, following the server's recipes. Each recipe asks for a level and a cost.
* **Forge**: Upgrades **equipped** gear up to **+10**. A failure destroys nothing: only the coins are lost.
* **Enchanting Altar**: Applies a scroll to equipped gear. The enchantment follows the item, and comes off for **750 coins**.
* **Sell**: Sells an item back from the bag.
* **Black market**: A personal selection at cut prices, open now and then: *it will reopen without warning, one day, at an hour nobody knows*.

## Earning without fighting

* **Daily**: the daily coin reward, on its own cooldown.
* **Fish**: 5 energy a cast, with a catch graded from *Common* to *Legendary*.
* **Travel**: set off for a destination for several minutes and run into events with choices. A trip must end before another can start.
* **Pay**: hand coins to a member, picked from the Discord selector. Never to yourself, never to a bot, and the server caps both the amount and the delay between two transfers.

## The campaign

A storyline in **five chapters**, each split into steps, with a recommended level and rewards per step and per chapter. It advances on its own from what you do: opening the tab can validate several steps at once - *you had already done what was needed*.

---

## Guild, village and war

An **RPG guild** is founded for **500 coins**, or joined by its name. It carries a level, a shared treasury and a member list; depositing coins into it makes it level up.

The **guild village** opens five buildings, to raise and upgrade out of the shared treasury. Each has three levels, asking for a rising guild level and cost:

* **Échoppe** (shop): A discount on the shop, up to -18%.
* **Forge de guilde** (guild forge): Upgrades that fail less often, up to +12% success.
* **Taverne** (tavern): Extra combat XP, up to +22%.
* **Caserne** (barracks): Stats granted to every member, up to +14 ATQ, +14 DÉF and +80 PV.
* **Chambre forte** (vault): Extra combat coins, up to +25%.

Only the guild leader builds, and each level states its effect and its cost before being paid for.

The **Clan war** tab ranks the teams by what the game brought them, over the **season** or over **this week**, and names the best fighters. It depends on what the server wired up: with the Clans module off, or the RPG bridge cut, Kotbo says so instead of showing an empty ranking.

## The weekly raid

Once a week a boss opens for a few hours. **Every team fights its own instance**, whose health follows its head count.

* The announcement carries the **Assault** button and each team's progress bar.
* Every member gets a limited number of assaults, and each assault costs energy.
* Rewards are shared: **30% evenly**, the rest in proportion to the damage dealt.

The raid closes at the planned time - or earlier if the clan season ends, so that its points still count.

## The Tower

  buttons="Enter, Shop, Leaderboard"

`/tower` (`/tour` in French) opens the RPG's roguelite mode: you climb floor by floor, with stats scaled down at the start of each climb (your class, its skills and your title always count). Every floor climbed earns **shards**, traded in the Tower's **Shop** for permanent upgrades or rewards.

* **Enter** starts a climb; **Resume** continues the current one, at the floor reached.
* Dying or **Leave the Tower** ends the climb and pays out the shards (only part of them if you die).
* **Leaderboard** shows the season's top, and a daily challenge runs alongside.

The channels where `/tower` is allowed are set like those of `/rpg` and `/raid`.

---

## Quests

The **Quests** tab lists what is open on the server, stating the scope - **personal** or **team** - and the deadline. Rewards are paid automatically as soon as the target is reached: there is nothing to claim.
