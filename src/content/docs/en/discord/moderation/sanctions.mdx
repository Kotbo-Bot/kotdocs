<p class="doc-lead">Every sanction goes through a single command, <strong>/sanction</strong>, and leaves a trace in the member's case file. What you do from Discord and what you do from the dashboard feed the same record.</p>

:::tip[On the dashboard]
The history, the sanction reports, the progressive scales and the archiving are covered on the [Sanctions](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/) page.
:::

:::caution[Subcommands keep their French names]
`/sanction` and its neighbours are not localised: the subcommands and options below are typed the same in both languages.
:::

## Issuing a sanction

<DiscordCommand
  command="/sanction warn membre:@Camille raison:Spam in #general gravite:Light"
  embedTitle="Warn recorded"
  embedDescription="Camille received a warning.\n\n**Reason**: Spam in #general\n**Total number of warns**: 2"
/>

* `/sanction warn`: Adds a warning. The **gravite** option weighs into the score when weighting is enabled.
* `/sanction to`: Applies a timeout, auto-renewed when the duration exceeds what Discord accepts.
* `/sanction kick`: Kicks the member from the server.
* `/sanction ban`: Bans permanently.
* `/sanction tempban`: Bans for a given duration.
* `/sanction softban`: Bans then immediately unbans, which deletes the member's messages from the **last seven days**.
* `/sanction tableau`: Applies the next step of a **progressive scale**, named in the command. The **bypass** option forces a specific tier.
* `/sanction list`: Lists a member's sanctions.

Every sanction takes a **raison** (reason), and durations are written plainly: `30m`, `2h`, `3d`, `1 week`.

Discord hides `/sanction` from anyone without the **Timeout Members** permission, and `/clear` without **Manage Messages**. Finer restrictions - per role, per channel - are set in [Command access](/en/dashboard/configuration/commandes/).

:::note[The context menu is faster]
Right-click **a member**, then **Apps → Sanction**, and the same dialog opens without typing the command. Kotbo refuses to warn a bot, to sanction yourself, or to target the bot.
:::

## Reading and annotating

* `/casier voir`: Opens a member's full case file. The **id** option looks up somebody who already left the server.
* `/note`: Writes or edits a member's moderation note, visible to the staff only. The **Moderator note** context menu does the same on a right-click.
* `/rescan`: Runs a fresh scan of the server's members.
* `/request-verification`: Asks a member to pass a security verification. They are timed out while it lasts. Context menu: **Request verification**.

The **View Case File** context menu, on a right-click **on a member**, opens the same record.

## Clearing a channel

<DiscordCommand
  command="/clear nombre:50"
  embedTitle="Messages deleted"
  embedDescription="**50** message(s) were deleted."
/>

`/clear` deletes a set number of messages in the current channel. The **membre** option limits the purge to one person's messages.

:::caution[Discord will not bulk-delete old messages]
Past fourteen days, Discord's API refuses bulk deletion. To archive a conversation before cleaning it, generate a transcript first.
:::

## Transcribing a conversation

* `/transcript generer`: Generates the current channel's transcript, over a **nombre** of messages or a **temps** window.
* `/transcript liste`: The server's latest transcripts.
* `/transcript rechercher`: Searches transcripts by channel name.
* `/transcript supprimer`: Deletes a transcript, administrators only.

Two context menus round it off, this time on a right-click **on a message**: **Transcribe from this message** and **Transcribe with context**.

Transcripts are then read back from the dashboard, on the [Transcripts](/en/dashboard/moderation/transcriptions/) page.

## The Kotbo actions context menu

A right-click opens **Apps → Kotbo actions**, a panel gathering the everyday gestures with no command to type. It comes in two flavours, depending on what you clicked, and only shows the actions your permissions allow.

### On a message

Open to everyone: **Traduire le message** (translate), **Sauvegarder en MP** (save to DM), **Donner de la réputation** (give reputation) and **Signaler ce message** (report).

Staff only:

* **Épingler / désépingler** (pin): requires *Manage Messages*.
* **Purger jusqu'ici** (purge up to here): requires *Manage Messages*.
* **Créer un thread** (create a thread): requires *Create Public Threads*.
* **Supprimer + avertir** (delete and warn): requires *Timeout Members*.

### On a member

Open to everyone: **Niveau et rang** (level and rank), **Infos du compte** (account info) and **Donner de la réputation** (give reputation).

Staff only:

* **Timeout rapide** (quick timeout), **Détection de double compte** (alt detection) and **Comptes liés** (linked accounts): require *Timeout Members*.
* **Renommer** (rename): requires *Manage Nicknames*.
* **Envoyer un MP via le bot** (DM through the bot) and **Historique d'invitation** (invite history): require *Manage Server*.

:::tip["Purger jusqu'ici" beats counting]
On a mess that dragged on, click the message where it started and purge up to there: no more guessing a message count for `/clear`.
:::

## Roles, channels and reports

* `/role add` and `/role remove` grant or remove a role to **several members** at once.
* `/channel` manages the server's channels from Discord.
* `/report` reports a member to the server staff; the **Report this message** context menu does the same from a message. The receiving channel and the delay between two reports are set in the [anti-raid](/en/dashboard/moderation/anti-raid/) page.
* `/signal` escalates a user to the bot's administrator, not to the server staff: keep it for cases that go beyond your community. Context menu: **Report this user**.
