<p class="doc-lead">The dashboard uses your Discord account to work out which servers you can access. There is no separate Kotbo account to create.</p>

## Sign in with Discord

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Open the dashboard</h3><p>Go to [the Kotbo Dashboard ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr) or to the address provided with your installation.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click “Sign in with Discord”</h3><p>Discord shows the OAuth authorisation page. Check that the account offered is the one you use on your server.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Authorise access</h3><p>Kotbo only reads the information needed to sign you in and to list the servers you can access.</p></div></div>
</div>

:::tip[Using several Discord accounts?]
Sign out of the wrong account from Discord in your browser, then start the dashboard sign-in again.
:::

## Choose the server to administer

Once you are signed in, the active server appears in the top left. Click its name to open the switcher. You can also use <span class="kbd">Ctrl</span> + <span class="kbd">G</span> (or <span class="kbd">⌘</span> + <span class="kbd">G</span> on macOS).

You only see the servers where Kotbo is installed and where your role has dashboard access.

## Adding Kotbo to a new server

The switcher only shows servers that are already equipped. At its bottom sits an **Add Kotbo to a server** entry (*See all my servers*), which opens the **My servers** page: it lists every server you administer, and offers an invite button on those that do not have the bot yet.

On a server that has just received Kotbo, the dashboard is not reachable yet: you are taken through the [setup journey](../parcours/), which builds the server and then opens the steering.

:::note[Without a subscription, the Free plan]
A server without a subscription is on the **Free** plan, with no time limit: moderation, logs, welcome, levels, tickets and statistics run as soon as the bot is added. The full plan, at a price decided by the server's size, opens the rest of the catalogue. See [Plans & billing](../../dashboard/configuration/facturation/).
:::

## Understanding your access level

<div class="role-grid">
  <div class="role-card"><b>Administrator</b><span>Access to configuration, modules and server management features.</span></div>
  <div class="role-card"><b>Moderator</b><span>Access to the operational tools you are allowed to use, without the sensitive settings.</span></div>
  <div class="role-card"><b>Staff member</b><span>Targeted access to the pages tied to their duties and their Discord roles.</span></div>
</div>

:::note
The sidebar adapts automatically to your permissions. A missing page usually means your role has no access to it, or that the matching module is not enabled.
:::
