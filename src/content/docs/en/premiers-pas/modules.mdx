## Enable a module

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Open Configuration → Modules</h3><p>This page is restricted to server administrators. You can [open Modules ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/modules) directly.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Find the feature</h3><p>Each card summarises what the module does. Flip the toggle next to it.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Configure its new page</h3><p>An entry appears in the sidebar. Open it to choose the module's channels, roles and behaviour.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Test it on Discord</h3><p>Run a simple action with a test account before announcing the feature to your community.</p></div></div>
</div>

## Where to start?

| What you need | Suggested modules |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome a community | Announcements & Auto-Role, Reaction Roles, Rules |
| Organise moderation | Members, Sanctions, Filters, Discord logs |
| Liven up the server | Leveling & XP, Giveaways, Suggestions |
| Structure a team | Tickets & Satisfaction, Recruitment, Planning, Directory |

:::caution[A module can sit outside your plan]
A card carrying a padlock and a **… plan** label is not disabled: it is not part of the server's plan, and its switch does not respond. A server without a subscription has **no** active module at all. See [Plans & billing](../../dashboard/configuration/facturation/).
:::

:::tip[Start small]
Enable two or three modules, configure them fully, then add the next ones. It makes testing easier and helps your staff get up to speed.
:::

## Disable a module

Go back to **Settings → Modules** and flip its toggle off. The matching page disappears from the sidebar and the related automations stop running.

Disabling a module should not be treated as deleting data. If you want to start over, check the module's own options first or contact the administrator of your Kotbo instance.

You can now configure your first module with the [Welcome & leave messages](../../dashboard/communaute/accueil-depart/) guide.
