<p class="doc-lead">Two pages at the bottom of the sidebar concern you alone: <strong>My profile</strong> gathers what the server knows about you, <strong>User settings</strong> tunes how the dashboard looks.</p>

:::note[Where to find them]
Below the navigation groups, above signing out: [My profile ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/profile) and [User settings ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/userSettings).
:::

## My profile

The tabs on show depend on who you are on this server.

| Tab | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff Space** and **Staff activity** | Members listed in the [staff directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/): rank, start date, tickets handled, sanctions issued, recent activity. |
| **Community** | Anyone with a public profile on the server: level, reputation, progression. |
| **My rank card** | You alone, on your own profile. |
| **API Keys & Security** | You alone, if you are staff and the server opens that access. |

The page is also where you write a **note** about yourself and, as a staff member, **submit a resignation** - the request goes to the team, it is not applied on the spot.

### My rank card

This is where the card `/rank` shows on Discord is set: background, pattern, avatar frame, XP bar, username font, up to three emojis and three badges. The achievements and what they unlock are detailed on the [Leveling & XP](/en/dashboard/xp/leveling/) page.

### API Keys & Security

An API key lets you plug an external tool into the server, with the list of permissions you tick when creating it.

:::danger[The key is shown once]
Copy it as it is created: it is never displayed in clear again. A key is **revoked** from the same page, and a revoked key stops answering immediately.
:::

## User settings

Two tabs: **Preferences** and **Profile widget**. Everything saves **automatically**, with no save bar.

### Appearance

* **Theme**, including a **custom theme** where you set the background, the surface, the text and the panels yourself, in light or dark mode.
* **Accent color**: violet, blue, green, pink, orange or cyan.
* **Compact mode**, which reduces margins and spacing, and **Interface animations**, which can be turned off.

### Language and region

* **Interface language**.
* **Date format**: *Relative* (3 days ago), *Absolute* (May 28, 2026) or *Both*.
* **Statistics time zone**: chart and heatmap hours are shown in this zone. *Automatic* follows your browser's.

### Navigation

**Sidebar behavior**: *Automatic* (collapses on small screens), *Always open* or *Always collapsed*.

### Notifications and privacy

* **Notification sounds** and **Desktop notifications** - the latter ask the browser for permission, and the **Test notifications** button checks that it all gets through.
* **Show my online status**, visible to other staff members.

### What you receive

The **What you receive** grid picks, for each of the nine notification categories, whether it reaches the **Dashboard**, arrives by **DM** on Discord, and whether those DMs are bundled into a **Daily summary** sent each morning. It is saved to your account and applies to all your servers. Details are on the [Notifications](/en/dashboard/general/notifications/#choosing-what-you-receive) page.

* Three links round off the block: the **privacy policy**, the details of **cookies and local storage**, and **Exercise my rights** - access, rectification, objection, restriction or erasure.

:::caution[Reset zone]
**Reset preferences** puts all these settings back to their default. It touches neither your rank card nor your server data.
:::

Your preferences can also be **exported** as JSON and **imported** back, for instance to find the same dashboard on another machine.
