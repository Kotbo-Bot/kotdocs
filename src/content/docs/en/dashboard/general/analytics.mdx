<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Statistics</strong> page measures what really happens on your server: messages, voice, posted content, members, joins and leaves, moderation, staff, community site. Every chart compares with the previous period, and alerts or reports keep you posted without opening the dashboard.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **General → Statistics** section: [Open statistics ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/analytics).
Collection stops when you turn off the **Analytics** module under **Settings → Modules**. Without it, nothing is recorded anymore.
:::

---

## Finding your way

On the left, a bar lists the **sections**; each has its sub-tabs at the top of the page. The bar **collapses** into icons (it does so on its own on a narrow screen) and expands with a click. Sections marked **New** are recent. At the bottom of the bar, **On their own page** leads to [Server health](/en/dashboard/general/pulse/) and [Invites](/en/dashboard/moderation/invitations/).

Each sub-tab has its own address: a shared link opens exactly the same view.

### The filter bar

* **Period**: **24 hours**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **365 days** or **Custom** (**From** / **To** dates, then **Apply**).
* **Compare with previous period**: overlays the previous period as a dotted line and shows the changes.
* A **channel or category** (*Whole server* by default) and a **role** (*All roles*).
* **Without staff** takes the team out of the figures; **Count bots** only changes the member count, since bots never post in activity stats.
* **Clear filters** goes back to the original view.

Some sub-tabs only follow the period, or have their own time window (live, for instance): a banner says so.

:::note[Which time zone hours are read in]
Hours are shown in **your** time zone, not the server's: you pick it in [your preferences](/en/dashboard/general/profil/), automatically (the browser's) or explicitly. It only changes the display, never the collected data.
:::

### Saved views

The **Views** menu saves the current tab, period and filters under a name, to come back in one click. **Share with the team** makes the view visible to everyone with access to statistics.

## Reading a chart

The main activity chart is driven by its figure cards: clicking a metric (messages, voice, active members…) shows it in the chart, with its **Total**, **Average**, **Peak** and **Minimum**.

* **Time step**: **Auto**, **Hour**, **Day**, **Week** or **Month**. Hourly steps only exist for the whole server, over 14 days at most.
* **Display**: **Line**, **Bars**, **Stacked by channel**, **Stacked by category** or **Table**.
* **Smoothing**: **Moving average**, **Previous period** and **Forecast** (which needs 28 days of history, at daily step, over a period ending today).
* Unusual days are flagged on the chart.

**Add a note** puts a dated marker on the charts ("tournament", "ad on another server"…). Clicking a point on the chart picks its date. Notes are visible to everyone on the team with access to statistics, with their author.

Rankings (members, channels…) switch between **Table** and **Bars**, with the change from the previous period. **See all** opens the full ranking, with search and export.

Clicking a member, a channel, a category or an invite opens its **card** in a window, with its charts, key figures and the same comparison with the previous period.

### Live

**Live** follows the server in real time, over the last 30 minutes: **Messages** and pace **Per minute (5 min)**, **Members writing**, **In voice**, **Online**, **Joins**, plus **Busy channels** and **Occupied voice channels**.

### Compare

**Compare segments** puts two or three **roles** or **channels** side by side over the chosen period, or the chosen period against another one (**Two periods**). Metrics: **Messages**, **Active members** or **Voice time**, day by day and as **Key figures**.

## Alerts and reports

### Threshold alerts

**New alert** tells you when a metric moves, for example "if messages drop 30% over a day":

* a **Name**, the **Metric**, the **Window**, the **Condition** and the **Threshold**; the **In plain words** sentence sums up the rule;
* a **Watched channel** (optional: whole server otherwise);
* the **Cooldown after trigger (hours)**, so you don't get the same alert over and over;
* recipients: a **Delivery channel** and/or **Staff members** **By direct message** (10 at most; a member with closed DMs receives nothing).

Alerts are checked every 5 minutes. **Recent triggers** compares the measured value with the usual value. A drop in the [Server health](/en/dashboard/general/pulse/) score can be watched with an alert too.

### Scheduled reports

**New report** sends a **Weekly** or **Monthly** summary - the past week or month, compared with the previous period - on the chosen **Day** and time, in the server's time zone. Pick its **Sections** and recipients, as for an alert. **Send a test** delivers it right away.

## Exporting

The **Export** button offers three formats:

* **Export CSV**
* **Export XLSX**
* **Export image**: generates an image of every chart visible on screen

:::caution[Data is needed]
An export over an empty period is refused with the message *No chart data to export*.
:::

## Stopping collection

Turning off the **Analytics** module from the [module catalogue](/en/dashboard/configuration/modules/) stops all recording: messages, voice minutes, reactions, threads, joins and leaves, per-member statistics, message content, exchanges between members, word frequency and usage rankings.

Other modules (moderation, tickets, levels…) keep working normally. Only the bot's technical measurements (run times and errors, with no identity) are kept.

:::note[What is never kept]
Content statistics only keep counts: message types, emojis, domains of linked sites - never the text of messages nor a link's full address. Exchanges between members are counted per pair and per day, without their content.
:::
