<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Pulse</strong> gives your server a health score every day, projects its growth and flags activity anomalies.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **General → Server health** section: [Open Pulse ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/pulse). The page is in **beta**.
It depends on the **Analytics** module: with no activity collection, there is nothing to analyse.
:::

Pulse is computed **every night** on the day that just ended. The **Recompute** button rebuilds the available history.

---

## Overview tab

The day's score is broken into five sub-scores:

* **Activity**
* **Moderation**
* **Growth**
* **Engagement**
* **Health**

Each score is rated **Excellent**, **Good**, **Average**, **Weak** or **Critical**.

The **metrics of the day** detail messages, voice, active members, joins and leaves, sanctions and resolved tickets.

:::caution[Today's score is provisional]
Until the day is over, Kotbo shows a partial score and says so explicitly.
:::

The **30-day trend** puts the day back in context.

## The bump reminder

The **Bump reminder** module has neither a page nor a setting: you read it here. Kotbo recognises the public replies of the known bump bots - **Disboard**, **Discadia**, **DiscordServers.com** and **Disforge** - and works out on its own when the server may bump again.

The banner on the Overview tab then reads *Next bump on … in N min*, or *Ready to bump on …* as soon as the window opens. At the same moment, Kotbo posts a **Bump reminder** embed in the **channel where the last bump happened**, recalling who did it, without pinging anyone.

:::note[Nothing to configure, but it needs a first bump]
The reminder only appears after a successful bump the bot has seen: that is what sets the service, the channel and the due time. A `/bump` refused for cooldown does not count - the bump bot answers ephemerally then, which Kotbo never sees.

The module is enabled in [Settings → Modules](/en/dashboard/configuration/modules/), *Integrations* category.
:::

## Alerts & recommendations

Pulse comments on the score rather than leaving it raw. Some of what it flags:

* *Very low activity, the server is dormant.*
* *High sanction rate, check the conflicts under way.*
* *The server is losing members, look into why.*
* *N leaves for M joins, retention is weak.*
* *Low engagement, few members take an active part.*
* *N open tickets waiting.*
* *N unhealthy channels detected.*
* *Excellent momentum, the server is in great shape!*

When the history is too short, Pulse says so: *Not enough data yet for a reliable diagnosis.*

## Predictions tab

### Growth forecast

Projection of the member count at **7 days** and **30 days**, with an average of members gained per day and a **confidence** rating. Low confidence signals a short or irregular history.

The chart distinguishes the **observed**, the **predicted** and an **80% interval**.

At least **five full days** of history are needed to project a trend.

### Seasonality

Kotbo identifies the **busiest day**, the **quietest day**, the **peak hour** and the **quiet hour** (in UTC). Below 14 days observed, the profile is flagged as not yet reliable.

### Anomalies detected

An abnormal **spike** or **drop** is reported with its date, its observed value and the expected value. This is the right place to find the day something happened without anyone noticing.
