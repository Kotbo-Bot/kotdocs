<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Notifications</strong> page gathers everything that concerns you, <strong>across all your servers</strong>: absence requests to approve, applications, security alerts, tickets, system messages. It replaces the former inbox.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **General → Notifications** section: [Open notifications ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/notifications). The old `/inbox` address leads there too.
The page does not depend on any module: everyone sees their own notifications there.
:::

---

## The three views

The tabs at the top sort the inbox:

| Tab | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Current notifications, read or not. |
| **Unread** | Only the ones you have not read yet. The tab's counter tallies them. |
| **Archived** | The ones you put away. They stay available. |

Two lists narrow the display down:

* **Server**: *All servers*, or a single one. The list also includes servers that sent you notifications even if you no longer have access to them.
* **Category**: *All categories*, or one of the nine - **Team**, **Planning**, **Recruitment**, **Moderation**, **Security**, **Tickets**, **Partnerships**, **Community** and **System**.

Notifications load in batches: **Load more** shows the next ones. When there is nothing, the page says *All clear*.

---

## Handling a notification

Each notification shows its category and its server. Several notifications of the same kind, close together in time, are **grouped** into a single line that reads *N in total*.

| Button | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Opens the item concerned in the dashboard, on the right server. |
| **Mark as read** / **Mark as unread** | Changes its state without moving it. |
| **Archive** / **Move back to inbox** | Puts it in the archives, or takes it out. |
| **Delete** | Removes it from your inbox. |

### Deciding without leaving the page

Some notifications carry a decision. An **absence request** and a **resignation request** show **Approve** and **Reject** right away: the decision goes through the module concerned, with its own permissions, then the notification moves to the archives.

At the top of the page, **Mark all as read** clears the counter (for the server picked in the filter, if any), and the refresh button reloads the list.

:::tip[The bell in the top bar]
The bell at the top of the dashboard shows the number of unread notifications and the latest ones. Its **All notifications** link leads to this page.
:::

## Choosing what you receive

The page's **Settings** button opens [My preferences](/en/dashboard/general/profil/#what-you-receive), where the **What you receive** grid sets three channels, category by category:

| Column | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | The notification shows up on this page and in the bell. |
| **DM** | Kotbo also sends it to you as a direct message on Discord. |
| **Daily summary** | The category's DMs arrive as a single message each morning. **DM** must be on. |

The grid is saved to your account: it applies to all your servers, and to the bot's DMs as well as the dashboard.

:::note[What always gets through]
What directly concerns you - a sanction, a decision on your request - arrives no matter what, even if the category is off.
:::

### Turning off a type of DM from Discord

Every notification DM carries a **Stop this type** button, except the ones that always get through. One click turns off DMs for that category; Kotbo confirms and reminds you that you can turn them back on from the dashboard. DMs of the same category sent a few moments apart are grouped into a single message.
