<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Members</strong> page is your server directory. Use it to find a member, review their activity and open their moderation file.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Moderation → Members** section: [Open the directory ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/members).
Access requires a moderation or administration role.
:::

---

## The indicators at the top

Four figures summarise the state of your community: **Total members**, then the number of members **present**, members who **left** and **bots**.

---

## Finding a member

The search field accepts a **name**, a **server nickname** or a **Discord ID**.

### Filtering

* **Humans** / **Bots**: restricts the list to one or the other.
* **On server** / **Left**: Kotbo keeps the record of members who left, so you can still look up the history of a departed account.
* **Reset the filters** clears everything.

### Sorting

Three sorts, each available ascending or descending:

* **Last activity**
* **Messages sent**
* **Join date**

The **Per page** selector controls how many rows are shown, and the counter at the bottom tells you how many members are visible out of the total.

---

## Reading a row

Each row shows the member, their **Activity** column (last activity, in minutes, hours or days, or *Never*) and their **Messages** column. Clicking a member opens their profile.

---

## A member's profile

Every member has their own page, at `/members/` followed by their ID: you can bookmark it or share it with staff. It also exists for a member who left, marked **Left**.

The header gives the join date, account creation, **Last activity** and inviter (**Invited by**), with **Copy ID**. Two buttons go further:

* **Full case file** opens the moderation case file in a window, with its tabs;
* **Moderate** applies a sanction straight away.

The profile then gathers:

| Block | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Points of attention** | What deserves a look: active sanctions, suspected alt account, pending reports, repeated warnings, account created shortly before joining, linked accounts, repeated joins, invitees who leave quickly. These badges are written in French. |
| **Staff note** | An internal note, changed with **Edit**. |
| **At a glance** | Messages and voice over 90 days, active days (with the current streak and the best), level, reputation received, balance, members invited (and how many are still here), tickets opened and their average rating, suggestions and forms, giveaways won. |
| **Roles** | The member's roles. |
| **Activity** | Messages per day, with a dot under the bar on voice days. |
| **Timeline** | Everything about the member, most recent first, filterable: **All**, **Joins**, **Moderation**, **Support**, **Community**, **Changes**. |
| **Risk level** | The number of warnings, the weighted score and how long ago the last sanction was. |
| **Where they talk** and **With whom** | Their most used channels and the members they talk with most. |
| **Linked accounts** | Alt accounts spotted, **validated** or **to check**. |

When [AegisAI](/en/dashboard/moderation/aegis/) is on, a card also sums up the tone of their messages.

:::tip[From other pages]
A member's case file also opens by clicking their name on the [Sanctions](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/), [Logs](/en/dashboard/moderation/logs/), [Suspicious accounts](/en/dashboard/moderation/double-comptes/) or [Tickets](/en/dashboard/communaute/tickets/) pages. The case file's **Full profile** link leads to this page.
:::
