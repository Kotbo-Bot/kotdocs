<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Security → Overview</strong> page analyses your server's whole configuration and gives it a grade. It is the recommended starting point before tuning anything in detail.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The page lives in the **Protection → Summary** section: [Open the overview ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/security).
Access is limited to administrators and authorised moderators.
:::

---

:::caution[This page is not translated]
The security overview is displayed in French whatever your dashboard language. Its labels are quoted verbatim below.
:::

## Reading the score

At the top of the page, a ring shows a **score out of 100** and a **grade** (letter). Below the ring, four counters break down the checks that were run:

* <span class="dash-badge kick">Critiques</span>: gaps to fix first (missing log channel, dangerous permissions left on `@everyone`…).
* <span class="dash-badge warn">Avertissements</span>: settings that could be better but do not put the server at immediate risk.
* <span class="dash-badge active">Informations</span>: convenience or usage notes.
* <span class="dash-badge active">Conformes</span>: checks that already pass.

Kotbo also tells you how many findings are **fixable in one click**, and how many score points that would earn.

The date and duration of the last analysis are shown under the score. Use the **Refresh** button at the top of the page to run a new audit.

---

## Score by category

The **Score par catégorie** block sorts categories from lowest to highest: the first row is the one to work on first.

Click a category to filter the findings list on it. Click again to return to the full list.

---

## Handling findings

The **Constats** block shows every check as a tile, with its severity, its category and an explanation. Critical findings get a wider tile.

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Filter</h3><p>Use the severity chips (<strong>Critiques</strong>, <strong>Avertissements</strong>, <strong>Informations</strong>, <strong>Conformes</strong>) to keep only what matters. A checkbox also lets you show or hide the checks that already pass.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Read the finding</h3><p>Each entry explains what was checked and, where relevant, the channels or roles involved.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Fix it</h3><p>When a finding offers a fix button, one click applies the setting without leaving the page.</p></div></div>
</div>

The **Tout activer** button applies the **risk-free** fixes in one go, after a summary of what is about to change (*Activer les recommandations*).

:::warning[Sensitive fixes]
Some fixes change existing server permissions: those keep their individual confirmation and stay out of the bulk action. Read the action label carefully, these changes apply to Discord immediately.
:::

A finding that points at undecided alt-account detections links straight to [Alt accounts](/en/dashboard/moderation/double-comptes/).
