<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Activity log</strong> keeps the history of configuration actions performed on your server. It answers the question "who changed this setting?".</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Moderation → Staff actions** section: [Open the log ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/activity).
This is a **core** module: it cannot be disabled from the Modules page, precisely so that no manoeuvre can erase its own trace.
:::

---

## The indicators

Three cards summarise the period on screen:

* **Actions**, subtitle *Configuration*: the number of recorded actions.
* **Modules**, subtitle *Sources*: the number of distinct modules involved.
* **Users**, subtitle *Unique*: the number of different people who acted.

---

## Browsing the log

The table shows the **Timestamp**, **User**, **Module**, **Action**, **Details** and **Type** columns.

* The **Search** field accepts an action, details, a module or a user.
* Column headers are sortable.
* The **Clear filters** button returns the table to its initial state.
* A counter shows how many events are displayed out of the total.

An action triggered by the bot itself carries the **Automatic** type; the others carry the name of the source that produced them.

If no row matches, Kotbo shows *No event matches your search*.

---

## What it is actually for

* Finding out **when** a log channel was changed, and by whom.
* Checking that a change announced by a staff member was really applied.
* Rebuilding the timeline after a configuration incident.

:::tip[Complementary to Discord logs]
The activity log covers actions **on the dashboard and the bot**. For events that happened **on Discord** (messages, sanctions, voice moves), see the [Discord logs & activity](/en/dashboard/moderation/logs/) page.
:::
