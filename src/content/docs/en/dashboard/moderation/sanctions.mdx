<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Security → Sanctions</strong> page centralises every warning, timeout, kick and ban issued on your server, together with its author, its target and its detailed report.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Protection → Sanctions** section: [Open sanctions ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/security/sanctions).
Viewing requires a moderation role; deleting an infraction is reserved for administrators.
:::

## History tab

### Browsing the list

The **Sanctions list** table shows the **Date**, **Target**, **Duration** and **Status** columns, along with the number of entries displayed out of the total.

* The search field accepts a type, a target, a staff member or a reason.
* The **Reset filters and sorting** button returns the table to its default state.
* Clicking a target opens their moderation file, from where you can apply a new action.

### Sanction reports

Depending on the sanction and your rights, the action button changes:

* **Create the report**: opens the form to complete for the selected sanction.
* **Add the evidence**: the report is pre-filled but is waiting for an evidence link (screenshot, message…).
* **View the report**: the report already exists and can be consulted.
* **Report reserved**: only the person who applied the sanction can create this report.

The **Sanction file** form asks for:

1. the **date and time of the incident**;
2. the **duration applied**;
3. the **rules broken**, picked from the articles of the Regulation module;
4. the **detailed reason** for the facts;
5. **additional notes** (context, history);
6. at least one valid **evidence link** in `http` or `https`.

:::caution[The regulation must exist]
If no article is configured, Kotbo refuses to create the report and points you to the **Regulation** module to add rules first.
:::

### Bulk actions

Tick one or more infractions - or use **Select all** - to act in one go. The bar shows how many entries are selected and offers **Clear selection**.

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Archive** | The infractions leave the active record and stop counting towards the warn score, but stay stored and readable. They then carry the *archived* badge. |
| **Unarchive** | They count again towards the warn score and automatic escalation. |
| **Lock** | The member will never be able to appeal these infractions again, even after the waiting period. *Locked* badge. |
| **Unlock** | The member will be able to request a review again. |
| **Delete** | The infractions disappear from the database and cannot be restored. |

:::caution[Archive rather than delete]
Deletion is permanent. Prefer archiving to take an infraction out of the active record while keeping a trace.
:::

### Deleting an infraction

Reserved for administrators, deletion is irreversible and requires typing the word **DELETE** to be confirmed.

## Configuration tab

* **Moderator role**: the role required to use the moderation commands.
* **Sanction propagation**: automatically applies sanctions on linked servers.
* **Sanction reports**: requires a detailed report for every sanction.
* **Ignore sanctions on bots**: sanctioning a bot produces neither a report nor a reminder - there is no victim to document and nobody to answer to. The sanction is still recorded in the member's file. Off by default.
* **DM the appeal link on a permanent ban**: sends the banned member the link to the appeal form. This setting is the same one as on the [Ban appeals](/en/dashboard/moderation/appels-ban/) page.

### Progressive sanction scales

A scale automatically escalates the sanction on each repeat of the same infraction.

1. Under **My scales**, create a scale named after the infraction (`Spam`, `Ads`, `Insults`…). Click its name to rename it.
2. Add **tiers**. For each tier, choose the action: **Warn**, **Temporary ban**, **Kick** or **Permanent ban**, together with a duration and a **custom reason** if the default reason does not fit.
3. Use **Insert a new tier** to slot in a step, and the delete icon to remove one.
4. A scale can be **exported** to be shared or archived.

On a repeat offence, Kotbo applies the next tier and records the scale name and the tier reached in the reason.
