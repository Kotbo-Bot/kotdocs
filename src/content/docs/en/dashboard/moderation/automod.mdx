## Available sanctions

Every filter is enabled on its own and has its own **Action** menu, which decides what Kotbo does when a rule is broken:

* **Delete silently**: the message disappears, with no notification to the member.
* **Delete and warn the member**: the message is deleted and the member receives a warning.
* **Block the message**: the message is blocked before it is posted.
* **Block and time out**: the message is blocked and the member is muted.
* **Timeout (10 min)**: the member cannot write for 10 minutes.
* **Warning**: a warning is recorded in their file.
* **Alert only (in the logs)**: nothing is deleted, an alert is simply sent to the logs.

## Available filters

### 1. Anti-spam
Limits how fast a member can post.
* **Maximum messages**: the allowed limit.
* **Within how many seconds**: the window used for the count.
* **Sanction**: the action applied when the limit is passed.

### 2. Links and invites
A single filter covers external links **and** invites to other Discord servers.
* **Allowed sites (one per line)**: any domain missing from this list triggers the sanction.

### 3. All caps
* **Share of capitals (%)**: the share of capital letters at which the message is sanctioned.
* **Minimum length**: below this character count the message is not analysed, which avoids blocking a plain "OK".

### 4. Too many emojis
Limits how many emoji a message may contain. Set to `0`, the filter is disabled and allows an unlimited number of emoji.

### 5. Too many mentions
Limits how many mentions a message may contain. Set to `0`, it allows an unlimited number of mentions.

### 6. Ghost pings
Detects pings that are deleted or edited quickly. Two actions to choose from: **Alert only** or **Warning and alert**.

### 7. @everyone and @here
Deletes unauthorized `@everyone` and `@here` mentions. Possible actions: **Delete only**, **Delete and warn**, or **Timeout (10 min)**.

### 8. Banned words
Your own blocklist.
* **Banned words (one per line, 1000 max)**.
* **Exceptions (allowed words, one per line)**: useful for a word that contains a forbidden sequence of letters.

### 9. Insults and offensive content
* **Active categories**: **Insults and profanity**, **Sexual content**, **Hate speech**.
* **Exceptions (words allowed anyway, one per line)**.

:::note[This filter relies on Discord]
The word lists are not maintained by Kotbo: the filter uses **Discord's predefined lists** to catch insults, sexual content and hateful terms.
:::

### 10. Adding bots
Prevents anyone other than the server owner from adding bots. A bot added by someone else is automatically kicked or banned.
* **People allowed to add bots**: the list of exceptions.
* **What to do with the added bot**: **Kick the bot** or **Ban the bot**.
* **Bot roles**: given to each bot as soon as it joins, instead of the automatic role. Integration roles, roles above Kotbo and roles granting *Administrator* are ignored.

To let a bot in once, the owner types `/bypass bot`: the chosen bot can join once within 10 minutes without being kicked, and the exemption is logged. Without an option, the command lists the bots currently allowed.

:::caution[Owner only]
Only the Discord server owner can enable, disable or configure this filter. Other staff members cannot touch it, even with full dashboard access.
:::

### 11. Emergency admin removal
Automatically removes ADMINISTRATOR roles when destructive actions are repeated in a short time. It is a safeguard against a compromised administrator account.
* **Short limit** and **Long limit**: the two rates that trigger the suspension.

### 12. Admin permission lock
Puts sensitive administration actions behind an approval step.
* **Roles that can approve**: in addition to the owner, members of these roles can approve or reject requests.
* **Alert channel**: where requests are announced.
* **View pending requests**: opens the queue.

:::caution[Owner only]
Only the server owner can enable, disable or configure Admin Permission Lock.
:::

:::warning[Role position]
Place the bot's role **above** the ADMINISTRATOR roles, otherwise the automatic reverts cannot be applied.
:::

## Servers & files tab

### Other servers

Blocks advertising for other servers without getting in your partners' way. Your own server always gets through, and members who can manage the server or messages are never targeted.

* **Invites**: including `ptb.` and `canary.` ones, and those hidden in an embed, a button or a forwarded message.
* **Forwarded messages and channel links**: a message forwarded from another server, or a link to one of its channels or events.
* **Displayed server tags**: the member is quarantined (Jail) while they display a targeted server's tag, and released as soon as they remove it.
* **Friend and group invites**.

Each item is set to **Follow the list**, **Always allowed** or **Always deleted**. The **Server list** works as a *Blocklist* (listed servers are refused) or an *Allowlist* (only listed servers get through): paste an invite (`discord.gg/partner`) then **Add**.

:::caution[Empty allowlist]
An empty allowlist refuses every invite to another server.
:::

### File extensions

Blocks families of files or only allows the ones you pick: **Executables**, **Images**, **Videos**, **Archives**, **Documents**, and up to 50 **Extra extensions**.

* **Server rule**: as a blocklist, ticked extensions are refused; as an allowlist, only they get through. An allowlist with no extension refuses every file.
* **Rules per channel or category**: a channel rule beats its category's, which beats the server's.
* **Always refuse disguised files**: an executable renamed `.png`, or an "image" Discord cannot read, is deleted everywhere, even without an allowlist.

**Sanction for deleted messages** applies to both blocks: delete only, warning, quarantine, kick or ban, one sanction per minute per member at most.

## Emojis tab

Bans specific emojis and stickers, or all of a server's. The message containing them is always deleted.

* **Sanction for new entries**: the sanction attached to the items you add next (warning, timeout, quarantine, kick, softban or ban). Each item keeps its own, editable in the list.
* **Ban a Discord emoji**: a native emoji.
* **Ban a server's emojis**: search a server Kotbo knows, then **Ban all** or click the emojis and stickers to ban one by one.

**Banned items** lists what is blocked; allowing an item again lifts the ban.

:::tip[From Discord]
On a message, right-click then **Apps → Kotbo actions → Interdire des emojis / stickers** offers the message's emojis and stickers, native ones included. The action requires the *Manage Server* permission.
:::

## Tags tab (tag automod)

Checks the server tag your members display next to their name, on join and on every change. Your own server's tag never triggers anything, and the staff (*Manage Server* or *Manage Messages*) is always exempt.

### Start from a template

On a server with no rule, the start screen offers ready-made templates that you adjust afterwards:

| Template | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Ticket, then kick** | A ticket asks the member to remove the tag; if it is still there 24 h later, kick. |
| **Ban a hostile server** | Anyone displaying that server's tag is banned on join. |
| **Block a word in tags** | Quarantine for any tag containing a word (*RAID*, *NUKE*…), whatever the server. |
| **Allow friendly servers** | Your partners' tags never trigger anything. |
| **Tag-based anti-raid** | A wave of joins with the same tag is quarantined and the tag blocked for 24 h. |
| **Kotbo shared list** | Quarantine for the tags of servers blocked by several Kotbo servers. |
| **Protect my tag** | Quarantine for fake tags imitating yours (Cyrillic letters, 0 for O…). |
| **Watch new accounts** | An alert when an account under 7 days old joins with another server's tag. |

### Rules

**New rule** targets a specific server or a tag text (contains, exact or regular expression) and picks the action, with optional escalation (*then kick after 24 h*). When several rules apply, the strongest response wins.

### Automatic detections

They spot suspicious behaviour without you having to name a server:

* **Wave of joins with the same tag**: several people join within a short time with the same tag. The **Threshold** is *Adaptive* (the *Soft*, *Normal* or *Strict* sensitivity is a floor, raised if the server normally gets many joins) or *Fixed*. **Then block the tag for** a duration, and **Report to other Kotbo servers** to feed the shared list (your server stays anonymous).
* **Mass adoption of a tag**: members already on the server take the same tag within a short time.
* **New account with a tag**: a brand-new account joins with another server's tag.
* **Tag imitating yours**: a fake tag almost identical to yours. Waits until your server has a tag known to Kotbo.
* **Kotbo shared list**: a server enters it once several different Kotbo servers have blocked or reported it.
* **Allowlist only**: only allowed tags get through, any other tag triggers the action.

Tags blocked after a wave show their end date; **Make permanent** keeps the block for good.

### Settings, test and log

* **Check on join** and **Check when a member changes tag**.
* **Alert channel**: if none is picked, alerts go to the logs channel.
* **Exempt roles**.
* **Test mode**: everything is written to the log and reported to the staff, nothing is applied. Handy to check a rule before letting it act.
* **Scan the server** runs current members through the configuration, shows how many would be affected, then **Apply now** handles each one, one person at a time.

The **Log** traces every decision, newest first, and **Recent waves** the latest detected waves with the follow-up given.

:::note[Quarantine needs the Jail role]
Without a Jail role set up in [Anti-raid → Quarantine](/en/dashboard/moderation/anti-raid/#quarantine-tab), tag quarantine becomes a timeout.
:::

## Testing a rule on past messages (Simulator tab)

<DashboardScreenshot
  file="dashboard-filters-simulator.webp"
  alt="Simulator tab of Kotbo's Filters page"
  caption="The Simulator: pick a rule, set it up, then replay it on past messages."
/>

The **Simulator** tab replays a rule on past messages without sanctioning anyone: you see which messages it would have hit before turning it on. A filter's **Test** button opens the simulator straight on that filter, with its settings.

1. Pick the **Rule type**: **Banned words**, **Invites**, **Caps**, **Emojis**, **Mentions**, **@everyone**, **Anti-spam**, **Scams** or **Custom regex**, then adjust its settings.
2. Pick the **Period**: **24 h**, **7 days** or **30 days**.
3. Tick **Compare with the live version** to see what your changes add or remove.
4. Click **Run simulation**.

The result shows **Messages hit**, **Members hit**, **Likely false positives** (staff, code or quote) and **Messages scanned**, with **Triggers per day**, **Most affected channels** and **Most affected members**. The message list highlights the triggering passage; **Allow this passage** adds it to the allow list.

When the result suits you, **Apply these settings to the page** carries the tested rule over to the filter. Remember to save afterwards.

:::note[An estimate]
The simulator replays the messages kept by [Logs](/en/dashboard/moderation/logs/): you need access to them. History keeps neither role mentions, nor the permission to mention @everyone, nor messages the bot deleted mid-spam: the result remains an estimate.
:::

## Exceptions / ignored channels and roles

You can globally exclude certain roles and channels from every filter, so AutoMod does not get in the way of your staff's work.

* **Ignored roles**: the roles filters never affect.
* **Ignored channels**: the channels where no rule is applied.

## Saving and Discord synchronisation

:::caution[Synchronisation required]
Kotbo's AutoMod partly relies on Discord's native AutoMod for the best performance. Whenever you change something on the dashboard:
1. Click **Save** in the bottom bar to store the settings.
2. The bot then synchronises the rules with your Discord server's security settings automatically. A warning appears on the dashboard if a rule could not be synchronised because of missing Discord permissions.
:::
