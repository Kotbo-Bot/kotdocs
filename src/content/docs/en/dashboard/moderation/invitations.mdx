<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Invites</strong> page answers one simple question: which channel really grows the server? It lets you name each link by source, measure attributed joins, and suspend an abusive inviter.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Moderation → Invites** section: [Open invites ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/invitations).
Kotbo needs the **Manage server** permission to read and delete invites.
:::

Four counters open the page: **Sources**, **Attributed joins**, **Total joins** and **Retention**. The **Refresh** and **Export** buttons sit in the top right.

---

## Invites tab

The table lists each link with its **Created**, **Code**, **Source**, **Inviter**, **Uses**, **Joins**, **Status** and **Last join** columns.

* The search field accepts a **code** or an **inviter**.
* The status filter offers **All statuses**, **Active**, **Suspended**, **Expired** and **Deleted**.
* A long-inactive link is marked <span class="dash-badge warn">Dormant</span>, and a link whose author is suspended carries the **Inviter suspended** badge.

On each row:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** / **Rename the source** | Assigns a source to the link (60 characters maximum). |
| **Copy the link** | Copies the `discord.gg/…` URL to the clipboard. |
| **Details** | Opens the link's record. |
| **Purge** | Kicks every member who joined through this code. |
| **Delete** | Permanently deletes the invite. |

:::danger[Purging really kicks people]
Purging an invite kicks every member who came through that link. Kotbo asks for confirmation and then reports how many members were removed.
:::

## Sources tab

This is where join attribution becomes readable.

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Name a first link</h3><p>Give it a meaningful name: <code>TikTok</code>, <code>Instagram</code>, a campaign, a partner.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Group them</h3><p>Reuse <strong>exactly the same name</strong> across several links to count them together.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Read the performance</h3><p>The <strong>Performance per source</strong> block ranks sources and shows, for each, the number of named links and the attributed joins.</p></div></div>
</div>

Kotbo shows the percentage of joins covered and reminds you how many invites are still unassigned. The **Best source** is highlighted.

Invites created by another bot - Disboard when bumping, a partnership bot… - automatically get a source named after it, in the form `Bot-Disboard`. Kotbo's own invites carry the name of the feature that created them.

## Top inviters tab

A ranking of members by the number of joins they brought in, with the date of their last join.

---

## Suspensions tab

### Suspend an inviter

Blocks a member's new invites, and can cascade-purge.

1. Enter the **Discord user ID** (required) and, optionally, their **tag** and a **reason**.
2. Tick **Cascade-purge the invited members** to also kick every member who came through their links.
3. Click **Suspend the inviter**.

### Suspended inviters

The list of active suspensions with their reason (or *No reason*). The **Restore** button reinstates the inviter.
