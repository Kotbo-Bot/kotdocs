<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Server rules</strong> module holds your server's rule articles, publishes them on Discord in a message kept up to date automatically, and can gate access to the server on accepting them.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Communication → Rules** section: [Open the rules ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/regulation).
The articles defined here directly feed the **Rules broken** field of [sanction reports](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/).
:::

---

## Writing the articles

The **New Rule** button opens the form:

* **Rule title** (required), for example *Respect and courtesy*.
* **Emoji** (optional), standard or custom.
* **Detailed description**, which supports Markdown.
* **Active and visible rule**: controls its public visibility and its synchronisation.

A live preview shows the result to the right of the form.

### Ordering

Articles can be reordered by drag and drop, or with the **Move Up** and **Move Down** buttons. The dashboard order is the published message order.

Each article carries a <span class="dash-badge active">Visible</span> or <span class="dash-badge">Hidden</span> badge, and the header shows how many articles are active out of the total.

### Deleting

Deletion requires typing a confirmation word.

:::danger[Effect on reports]
Deleting an article removes it from **all future sanction reports**. Reports already written keep their reference.
:::

---

## Publishing on Discord

1. Pick the **Publish Channel**. With no channel, Kotbo refuses to publish.
2. Click **Publish rules**.
3. After a change, **Update message** refreshes the existing message rather than creating a new one.

**Automatic synchronisation** keeps the Discord message aligned with the dashboard list. Every publication is announced in the staff announcement channel; no member is notified by direct message.

---

## Verification through the rules

This option adds an acceptance button under the rules message.

* **Verification**: turns the button on.
* **Assigned role**: the role granted on acceptance. Leave it empty for Kotbo to create a `Vérifié` role automatically.
* **Lock**: hides the other channels until the member accepts.

:::warning[Mind the permissions]
Enabling verification can create Discord permission conflicts and inadvertently give `@everyone` read access to some private channels. Check that your private channels **explicitly deny** the read messages permission to `@everyone` before turning this on. Kotbo shows this warning and asks for confirmation.
:::
