<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Fun channels</strong> module creates playful, automated spaces on your Discord server. Kotbo enforces the rules and keeps score in real time for several classic games.</p>

:::tip[On the Discord side]
To understand the game rules and the player experience on Discord, read the [Fun channels gameplay guide](/en/discord/communaute/salons-fun).
:::

:::note[Availability]
These game channels are configured from the **Community → Fun channels** section of your dashboard: [Open the Fun channels module ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/fun).
Changes require Kotbo to have administrator permissions on the server.
:::

## Counting channel

The counting game invites your members to count as high as they can, in ascending order (1, 2, 3, 4, and so on).

### Setting up the channel
1. Go to **Community → Fun channels**.
2. In the first block, **Counting Channel**, open the **Discord channel** dropdown and pick the channel dedicated to the game.
3. The block shows, in real time:
   * **Count**: the current score of the counting chain.
   * **Last player**: the ID of the last user who posted a valid number.
4. If a member makes a mistake or you want to reset the game, click **Reset the count** (a confirmation message appears).

:::note[Two numbers posted at once]
Only one of them counts: the losing message is deleted with a warning, **without breaking the chain**. The same rule protects every game channel, whose messages are handled in order, channel by channel.
:::

## One Word Story

This collaborative game invites the community to write a shared story, one word at a time.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the middle block, **One Word Story**, select the text channel dedicated to the story from the **Discord channel** dropdown.
2. The status area shows the ID of the **last author** who added a word.
3. This channel is entirely policed by the bot: invalid messages are deleted and double posts are blocked without any moderator involvement.

## Mystery Number

Challenge your members to guess a secret number generated at random by the bot.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the third block, **Mystery Number**, select the game's text channel from the **Discord channel** dropdown.
2. The block shows the **Mystery target** (visible only to staff, on the dashboard).
3. To change the target value or start a new round manually, click **Generate new target**. A *Generate a new mystery number?* confirmation appears before it applies.

## Word Chain

Each word must start with the previous word's last letter.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the **Word Chain** block, pick the dedicated channel from the **Discord Channel** dropdown.
2. The block shows the **Last word** played and the **Last player**.
3. The **Reset the chain** button starts over, after confirmation.
4. Words with ligatures (`cœur`, `œuvre`) are accepted, and a word cannot end with a hyphen.

## Emoji Riddle

Members guess the phrase hidden behind a string of emojis.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the **Emoji Riddle** block, pick the dedicated channel.
2. The block shows the **Current clue**: it is the same string of emojis the bot posts in the channel, not a secret.
3. The **New riddle** button generates another one, after confirmation. Kotbo announces it in the channel right away, as it does after every correct answer.

### Writing your own riddles

Below the channel, the **Server riddles** section holds your own list: they are drawn at random in the channel, just like the ones Kotbo ships. The counter shows your total out of a maximum of **200**.

The **Include built-in riddles** switch mixes yours with Kotbo's. Turned off, only yours are drawn - unless you have none. The **Show built-in riddles** link unfolds the default list.

:::tip[Several wordings beat one]
A riddle only counts as solved if the typed answer is in the list. Plan for the common variants (with and without the article, singular and plural) instead of relying on a single phrasing.
:::

## Never Say Yes/No

No answering yes or no.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the **Never Say Yes/No** block, pick the dedicated channel.
2. The channel is moderated automatically: any message containing the French words "oui" or "non" is deleted.

---

## Emoji Only

Only emojis are allowed in the channel.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the **Emoji Only** block, pick the dedicated channel.
2. The channel is moderated automatically: any message containing anything but emojis is deleted. Server emojis, skin tones, flags and keycap digits all get through.

---

## Image of the day

One image per member per day: everyone posts the image they like best today, from 00:00 to 23:59 server time.

### Setting up the channel
1. In the **Image of the day** block, select the dedicated channel.
2. This channel is moderated automatically: a message without an image, or a second image the same day, is deleted, with a message explaining why. Staff are not filtered.

---

## Punitive mode

A single switch, shared by every game, decides what happens on a mistake.

* **On**: a counting or word-chain mistake **resets the game to zero**.
* **Off**: the offending message is deleted and its author warned, **without breaking the progress**.

---

## Saving your changes

:::caution[Saving required]
As soon as you change a fun channel assignment, an action bar appears at the bottom of your screen. You must click **Save** for the changes to take effect on your Discord server. Click **Reset** if you want to discard your pending changes.
:::
