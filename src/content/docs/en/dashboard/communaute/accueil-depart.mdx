## Configure the welcome message

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Open the “Welcome” tab</h3><p>In <strong>Announcements & Auto-Role</strong>, the tab sits just below the page title.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Turn on “Welcome Message”</h3><p>The toggle is in the top right of the block.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Choose the channel to post in</h3><p>Pick a text channel that new members can see, for example <code>#welcome</code>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Write the message</h3><p>Add the variables below so the text adapts automatically to each arrival.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Check the preview, then save</h3><p>The Discord preview refreshes on the page. Click <strong>Save</strong> in the bottom bar.</p></div></div>
</div>

## Available variables

<table class="variable-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Variable</th><th>Result</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><code>{'{user}'}</code></td><td>Mentions the member, for example <strong>@JohnDoe</strong>.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{username}'}</code></td><td>Shows their name without a mention.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{server}'}</code></td><td>Shows your server's name.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{memberCount}'}</code></td><td>Shows the total number of members.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Example:

```text
Welcome {user} to {server}!
You are our member number {memberCount}!
```

## Add a welcome image

Turn on **Welcome image** to attach a banner featuring the new member's avatar. You can supply a custom background image URL; without one, Kotbo uses its default artwork.

:::tip
Choose a wide, legible and uncluttered image. Test how it looks in both light and dark themes if it contains text.
:::

## Configure the leave message

Open the **Leave** tab at the top of the same page. The form works the same way: enable the message, choose a channel and write the text. The <code>{'{user}'}</code> variable may no longer produce a clickable mention once the member has left; use <code>{'{username}'}</code> if you only want to show the name.

## The other tabs on the page

The **Announcements & Auto-Role** page is not limited to welcome and leave: it has five tabs.

### Auto-Roles

Three automatic role assignments, independent from each other. Each one can be left on **None (Disabled)**.

* **Join Auto-Role**: assigns a role to any new member joining the server.
* **Server Tag Auto-Role**: assigns a role to members displaying the server's official tag on their Discord profile. The role is removed as soon as the member drops the tag.
* **Presence Keyword Auto-Role**: assigns a role based on what the member shows in their Discord presence.
  * **Keyword to detect**: the text being searched for, for example `.gg/kotbo`.
  * **Where to look**: **Custom status**, **Current activity**, or **Both**.

### Boosts

The **Custom Boost Announcements** tab lets you publicly thank members who boost the server, with its own message and its own channel. The **Enable boost image** option attaches a generated visual.

### Welcome Thread

Kotbo can open a private thread for each new member and run a welcome script inside it.

* **Message sequence**: the messages sent in order, before the final menu. Every message in the sequence must have content, otherwise saving is refused. With no sequence, the bot goes straight to the final menu.
* **Menu introduction pages**: each page becomes a button or an option in the final menu. With no page configured, the final menu shows no buttons.
  * **Click action**: **Display embed**, **Manage role**, or **Link**.
* **Typing indicator**: displays "is typing" before each message, for a more natural feel.
* **Auto-archive**: closes the thread after the chosen period.
