<p class="doc-lead">Welcome new members, see off those who leave and thank boosters with personalised messages, hand out automatic roles, and compare several welcome messages to keep the one that makes the most people stay.</p>

:::tip[On the Discord side]
To see how these messages look on Discord and to test your configuration, read [How the messages render on Discord](/en/discord/communaute/accueil-depart).

In the sidebar, open **Communication → Welcome & goodbye** ([Open Welcome & goodbye ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/announcement)). The page has six tabs: **Welcome**, **Goodbyes**, **Boosts**, **Auto roles**, **Welcome thread** and **A/B tests**.

## Setting up the welcome message

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Open the "Welcome" tab</h3><p>It sits right under the page title.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Turn on "Welcome Message"</h3><p>The switch is at the top right of the block.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Pick the broadcast channel</h3><p>Select a text channel new members can see, for example <code>#welcome</code>.</p></div></div>

## Available variables

<table class="variable-table">
  <thead><tr><th>Variable</th><th>Result</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><code>{'{user}'}</code></td><td>Mentions the member, for example <strong>@JohnDoe</strong>.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{username}'}</code></td><td>Shows their name without a mention.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{displayName}'}</code></td><td>Shows the nickname they use on the server.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{server}'}</code></td><td>Shows your server's name.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{memberCount}'}</code></td><td>Shows the total number of members.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>{'{boostCount}'}</code></td><td>Shows the current number of boosts.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Example:

```text
Welcome {user} to {server}!
You are our member number {memberCount}!
```

## Adding a welcome image

Turn on **Enable welcome image** to attach a banner with the new member's avatar. The **Custom background image URL (Optional)** field changes the background; without a URL, Kotbo uses its default artwork.

:::tip
Pick a wide, readable, uncluttered image. If it contains text, check how it renders in both light and dark themes.
:::

## Setting up the leave message

Open the **Goodbyes** tab. The form works the same way: turn on the **Leave Message**, pick a channel and write the text. The <code>{'{user}'}</code> variable may no longer produce a clickable mention after the member has left; use <code>{'{username}'}</code> if you only want to show the name.

## The page's other tabs

### Boosts

The **Boosts** tab holds **Custom Boost Announcements**: thank members who boost the server publicly, with its own message and its own channel. **Enable boost image** attaches a generated banner, whose background can be changed through a URL.

### Auto roles

Three automatic role assignments, independent from one another. Each can stay on **None (Disabled)**.

* **Join Auto-Role**: gives a role to every new member who joins the server.
* **Server Tag Auto-Role**: gives a role to members who wear the server's official tag next to their name. Discord itself says who wears it: nothing to type. The role is removed as soon as the member drops the tag.
* **Presence Keyword Auto-Role**: gives a role based on what the member shows on Discord, and removes it when the keyword disappears.
  * **Keyword to detect**: the text to look for, for example `.gg/kotbo`.
  * **Where to look**: **Custom status**, **Current activity**, or **Both**.
  * The role can be **Same as the tag auto-role**, or a separate one.

:::note[Members already on the server]
An auto-role only applies to members whose tag or status changes after it is enabled. **Rescan members** catches up with those who were already there.
:::

### Welcome thread

When a member joins, Kotbo opens a thread just for them, sends a few messages as if typing them, then shows them around the server.

* **Thread parent channel**, **Thread mode** (**Public** or **Private**), **Thread name template** and **Auto-archive**.
* **Typing indicator**: shows "is typing" before each message.
* **Delete inactive threads**: removes welcome threads with no activity after the **Inactivity delay (hours)**, so they don't pile up.
* **Default name and avatar** for the messages; each message in the sequence can override them.
* **Scripted message sequence**: messages sent in order, before the final menu, each with its **Delay before sending**. Each message needs content, or saving is refused. Without a sequence, Kotbo goes straight to the final menu.
* **Final introduction menu**: a message (title, description, colour, images) with **Buttons** or a **Dropdown menu**.
* **Menu introduction pages**: each page becomes a button or option in the final menu. Without pages, the menu shows no buttons.
  * **Click action**: **Display embed**, **Manage role** or **Link**. A link leads to a server channel or an external URL; in **Buttons** style, Discord then shows a real link button.
  * For a role, the **Behavior** is **Add**, **Remove**, **Toggle** or **Exclusive**: an **Exclusive group** automatically replaces any other role from the same group.

### A/B tests

**Welcome A/B tests** tries several welcome messages at once: each newcomer gets a random version, and Kotbo compares who stays and who starts talking.

**New test** asks for:

* a **Name** and a **Hypothesis**, to remember in a month what you expected;
* the **Primary metric**, the one that picks the winner: **Still here on day 1**, **Still here on day 7**, **Still here on day 30** or **Talked in the first week**;
* at least two versions, each with a **Version name**, a **Weight** (its share of newcomers), a **Welcome message** (empty: the current message from the Welcome tab), the **Welcome card** (with, without, or same as configuration) and the **Welcome thread**.

**Start** launches the test, **Stop** ends it. **Results** compares versions: newcomers, messages in week 1, and the chance of beating the **Control** version. A newcomer only counts once its measurement delay has passed.

A version is declared the winner above a 95% chance, with at least 30 newcomers measured per version. **Apply** makes it the server's welcome message.

:::caution[An active welcome message is required]
If the welcome message is disabled or has no channel, a test changes nothing: set up the **Welcome** tab first.
:::
