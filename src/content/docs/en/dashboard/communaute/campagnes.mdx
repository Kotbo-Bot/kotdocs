<p class="doc-lead">A <strong>campaign</strong> chains several messages around a date: a teaser, the announcement, a follow-up. Each one goes out on its own at its due time, in a channel or as a direct message.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Communication → Campagnes** section: [Open campaigns ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/campaigns). The page is in **beta**.
:::

:::caution[This page is not translated]
Campaigns is displayed in French whatever your dashboard language. Its labels are quoted verbatim below.
:::

## Following your campaigns

Every campaign gets a card, with its status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Brouillon** | Nothing is scheduled. This is the only editable state. |
| **Planifiée** | Launched: the sends will go out at their due time. |
| **En cours** | At least one step has been sent. |
| **Terminée** | Every step has gone out. |
| **Annulée** | Stopped along the way; messages already posted stay in place. |

The card shows how many steps were sent, the pivot date, the partner servers targeted, then the **step timeline**: offset, delivery mode and the start of the message, with the number of recipients served and failures for those already sent.

On a running or completed campaign, four figures are computed: **Envoyés**, **Échecs**, **Réactions** and **Arrivées**.

## Creating a campaign

The **Nouvelle campagne** button opens an editor in four blocks: what you say, to whom, and how you will know whether it worked.

### General

* **Nom** of the campaign.
* **Date pivot (jour J)**: the date every offset is counted from.
* **Description interne**: seen by the staff only.

### Steps

A step is one message and its moment. The **Décalage (min)** is counted in minutes from the pivot date, and it accepts negatives: `−10080` min places the step one week before D-day. The timeline spells that offset out (`J−7 j`, `Jour J`, `J+2 h`).

Every step picks its **Diffusion**:

* **Dans un salon**: the message is posted in the chosen channel.
* **En message privé**: the message is sent to the computed audience by DM.

In the text, `{server}` is replaced by the server name, and `{user}` by the recipient in direct messages.

### Audience

The criteria stack, and they only apply to **direct message steps**: a channel message is seen by everyone with access to it.

* **Rôles visés** and **Rôles exclus**.
* **Niveau minimum**, **Ancienneté (jours)**, **Inactif depuis (jours)**.

The **Compter** button shows how many members match and a few names, without sending anything.

### Measurement & partners

* **Code d'invitation à créditer**: joins that went through this code since the pivot date are counted as conversions. Without it, no join can be attributed to the campaign, and the *Arrivées* figure stays empty.
* **Diffuser aussi sur (IDs de serveurs)**: partner servers where Kotbo is present. Channel steps are posted there if a channel with the same ID exists; unreachable servers count as failures.

## Launching, cancelling, deleting

The **Lancer** button asks for confirmation, recalling the number of steps and, if any, those going out as direct messages.

:::caution[A missed send is not caught up]
Sends go out automatically at their due time. A step whose time has already passed at launch will not be replayed.
:::

* **Annuler** stops a scheduled or running campaign. It can then be moved back to draft with **Repasser en brouillon**.
* **Supprimer** erases the campaign and its measurements. Messages already posted stay in your channels.
