---
title: Highlighted messages
description: Repost the messages people react to in a dedicated channel, from a vote score you define.
---

<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Starlight</strong> module watches the reactions placed on your messages and reposts as highlights those that pass a vote score, in a channel you choose.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Community → Highlighted messages** section: [Open Starlight ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/starboard). The module is in **beta** and **off by default**.
:::

:::caution[No channel, nothing published]
*Without a publishing channel, Starlight reposts nothing.* It is the only mandatory setting: while it is missing, the module stays idle even when enabled.
:::

---

## Publishing

* **Highlights channel**: where highlighted messages are reposted.
* **Highlight threshold**: the score (upvotes minus downvotes) required to get reposted.
* **Embed color**: the colour of the published highlight.

---

## Vote scale

* **Upvote emojis**: at least one is required. The first one is used as the highlight counter.
* **Downvote emojis**: subtracted from the score. Leave empty to disable downvoting.
* **Count reactions on the highlight**: votes cast on the reposted message feed the same score. That is often where members vote.

Emojis can be typed in or picked among the server's own; a custom emoji is also accepted by its ID.

---

## Seeding reactions

Add the vote emojis upfront, so nobody has to look for them.

* **Seed highlights**: the bot adds the vote emojis to every reposted message.
* **Channels to seed**: every new message there gets the vote emojis. This is what turns a channel into a suggestion box.

---

## Scope

* **Watched channels**: leave empty to watch the whole server.
* **Excluded channels**: always take precedence over watched channels.
* **Include bot messages**: takes messages posted by bots and webhooks into account. Off by default.

---

## Lifecycle

**Remove below threshold**: the highlight is deleted if the score falls back under the threshold. Otherwise it stays published once reached.

---

## Saving

Changes go through the save bar at the bottom of the screen. Kotbo confirms with *Starlight configuration saved*.

The module switch sits at the top of the page, as on every module page.
