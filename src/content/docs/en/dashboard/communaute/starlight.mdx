title: Highlighted messages
description: Repost the messages people react to in a dedicated channel, from a vote score you define.
---

<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Starlight</strong> module watches the reactions placed on your messages and reposts as highlights those that pass a vote score, in a channel you choose.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Community → Highlighted messages** section: [Open Starlight ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/starboard). The module is in **beta** and **off by default**.
:::

:::caution[No channel, nothing published]
*Without a publishing channel, Starlight reposts nothing.* It is the only mandatory setting: while it is missing, the module stays idle even when enabled.
:::

---

## Overview tab

The **Module status** panel sums up the settings (vote type, threshold, reward, tiers, safeguards) and activity: messages shown in the channel and the last time a message was featured. Kotbo flags what is blocking: no publishing channel, deleted channel, **The bot is missing permissions** (view the channel, send messages, embed links, add reactions), or **Lots of featured messages** when too low a threshold makes the channel unselective.

* **Top messages**: the gallery of the best-scored messages over the period.
* **Featured messages**: the whole history, filterable by channel, **Period** and member, sorted by **Most recent** or **Best score**. Each entry links to the **Original message** and its **Repost**.

The staff can **Remove this message from the featured channel** (it never comes back and its reward is taken back; **Restore** undoes it) or **Exclude this member from the featured channel**.

---

## Settings tab

### Publishing

* **Highlights channel**: where highlighted messages are reposted.
* **Highlight threshold**: the score (upvotes minus downvotes) required to get reposted.
* **Embed color**: the colour of the published highlight.
* **Per-channel thresholds**: a higher threshold in a very active channel, a lower one in a quiet channel. A thread follows its parent channel.

The **Message preview** shows the repost as it will appear.

---

### Vote scale

* **Upvote emojis**: at least one is required. The first one is used as the highlight counter.
* **Downvote emojis**: subtracted from the score. Leave empty to disable downvoting.
* **Count reactions on the highlight**: votes cast on the reposted message feed the same score. That is often where members vote.
* **Ignore the author's vote**: their reaction on their own message, or on its highlight, doesn't count in the score.

Emojis can be typed in or picked among the server's own; a custom emoji is also accepted by its ID.

---

### Seeding reactions

Add the vote emojis upfront, so nobody has to look for them.

* **Seed highlights**: the bot adds the vote emojis to every reposted message.
* **Channels to seed**: every new message there gets the vote emojis. This is what turns a channel into a suggestion box.

---

### Scope

* **Watched channels**: leave empty to watch the whole server.
* **Excluded channels**: always take precedence over watched channels.
* **Include bot messages**: takes messages posted by bots and webhooks into account. Off by default.

---

### Safeguards

* **NSFW channels protected**: a message from an NSFW channel is never reposted in an all-ages channel, whatever its score. It is only if the featured channel is NSFW too.
* **Maximum message age (days)**: an older message no longer gets featured. `0`: no limit.
* **Excluded members**: their messages are never featured; those already featured are removed on save.

### Rewards

* **Author XP** and **Author coins**: what the author gets the first time a message is featured. Taken back if the staff removes the message.
* **Notify the author privately**: a DM tells them their message is featured, with what they earned. Sent once per message.
* **Role tiers**: **Add a tier** gives a **Role given** at a number of **Featured messages**. A message removed by the staff no longer counts and can cost a tier. Kotbo flags roles it can't give.

[Triggers](/en/dashboard/communaute/declencheurs/) can also react to a message being featured.

### Lifecycle

**Remove below threshold**: the highlight is deleted if the score falls back under the threshold. Otherwise it stays published once reached.

---

## Saving

Changes go through the save bar at the bottom of the screen. Kotbo confirms with *Starlight configuration saved*.

As on every module page, you turn it on from the **Module disabled** banner and off from the page's options menu, **Turn off module** entry.
