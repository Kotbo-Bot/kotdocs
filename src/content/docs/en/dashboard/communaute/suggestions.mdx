<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Suggestions</strong> module lets your members submit ideas for the server, vote on them (for or against) on Discord, and lets your team answer from the dashboard.</p>

:::tip[On the Discord side]
To see how members submit ideas and vote on Discord, read the [Discord guide to Suggestions](/en/discord/communaute/suggestions).
:::

:::note[Availability]
Section **Community → Suggestions**: [Open the Suggestions module ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/suggestions).
Members suggest with `/suggest`, the channel button or the [server site](/en/dashboard/communaute/site/). Deciding on a suggestion requires moderator or administrator permissions.
:::

---

## To review tab

The **Module status** panel sums up the settings (decision comment, votes after approval, who can suggest, vote threshold, safeguards) and the latest suggestion. Kotbo adds advice: no channel picked, suggestions waiting for a long time, or no new suggestion for several days.

* **Queue**: suggestions waiting for a decision, sorted by **Most supported**, **Oldest** or **Newest**. The **Threshold reached** and **Waiting for a while** badges flag the ones to look at first.
* **By category**: how suggestions split across your categories.
* **All suggestions**: the history, filterable by status and category.

Each suggestion shows its score (for votes minus against votes), the staff's answer if any, **View on Discord** and the **Discussion** when a thread is open. With **Voters visible to staff**, the **Voters** button lists who voted for and against. Clicking the author opens their profile.

### Deciding

**Decide** opens the decision: **Pending**, **Approved**, **In progress**, **Implemented** or **Rejected**, with a **Public answer** shown under the suggestion on Discord. Then **Save the decision**.

A decision can be revisited with **Revise**. **Set back to pending** removes the published answer and reopens voting.

Kotbo updates the message on Discord (colour, status, answer and the moderator's name), posts the decision in the thread and, if **Notify the author** is on, sends it to them by DM.

## Settings tab

### Publishing

* **Suggestions channel**: where suggestions sent with `/suggest`, the channel button or the site are posted. If none is picked, they go to the server's public channel, or to a channel whose name contains "suggest".
* **Discussion thread**: opens a thread under each new suggestion to discuss it without cluttering the channel. The decision is posted there too.
* **Message preview**: the suggestion as it will appear, for each status.

### Decisions

* **Public answer required**: every decision must come with an explanation members can see.
* **Voting stays open after approval**: members keep voting on an approved suggestion until it is in place.
* **Notify the author**: they get the decision and the answer by DM. If their DMs are closed, they are mentioned in the thread.
* **Voters visible to staff**: the staff sees who voted what. Members still only see the counters.

### Reward

**XP** and **Coins** the author gets when their suggestion is approved, in progress or implemented. They are paid once, and taken back if the decision is undone or the suggestion deleted.

### Categories

Add a **New category**: members pick one when suggesting, in the window as in `/suggest`.

### Vote threshold

**"For" votes needed**: a suggestion reaching it is flagged to the staff, and stays flagged even if votes are withdrawn. **Pin the message** pins it in the channel when the threshold is reached, and unpins it when the staff decides. [Triggers](/en/dashboard/communaute/declencheurs/) can fire at the threshold too.

### Who can suggest and safeguards

* **Allowed roles**: empty, everyone can suggest. Server managers always can.
* **Delay between two suggestions** (in minutes), **Minimum account age** and **Minimum time on the server** (in days): against bursts of suggestions and accounts created for the occasion. `0` for no requirement.

### Updating published messages

**Update** reformats the last 100 suggestions on Discord, handy after changing a setting.

Settings are saved with the bar at the bottom of the screen; the tab shows *Needs fixing* while a field is invalid.
