<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Reaction roles</strong> module posts a message with buttons on Discord. One click grants the associated role; depending on the chosen mode, a second click removes it, or taking another one removes the previous one.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Community → Reaction roles** section: [Open reaction roles ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/reaction-roles).
Kotbo needs the **Manage roles** permission, and its own role must sit **above** the roles it grants in the server hierarchy.
:::

---

## Deploy a panel

**Deploy a Panel** opens a four-step window, with the **Preview on Discord** in a folding panel. On a server with no menu, the page offers the templates straight away.

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Start from a template (optional)</h3><p><strong>Colors</strong> (one name colour at a time), <strong>Pronouns</strong>, <strong>Notifications</strong>, or <strong>Blank</strong>. The template fills the menu with suggested roles; nothing is created until you publish.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>How many roles can a member take?</h3><p>See <a href="#the-three-modes">the three modes</a>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>The buttons</h3><p>For each: an <strong>Emoji</strong> (optional), the <strong>Button text</strong> and the <strong>Role given</strong>. <strong>Add a button</strong> creates more; the handle reorders them by drag and drop, or with the up and down arrows.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Where to publish</h3><p>Title, <strong>Destination Channel</strong> and <strong>Message text</strong> (empty: Kotbo keeps the text explaining how to take the roles). Then <strong>Deploy Panel</strong>.</p></div></div>
</div>

A role suggested by a template that doesn't exist yet is created on publish, with no permission at all; Kotbo tells you how many roles it will create. **Pick an existing role instead** swaps it for one of the server's roles.

Closing the window (or **Esc**) before publishing asks for confirmation: *Close without publishing* clears what you had prepared.

:::caution[Text and role required]
Every button needs a text **and** a role, otherwise Kotbo refuses to publish.
:::

## The three modes

| Mode | What a click does |
| --- | --- |
| **Several** | The member takes and removes the roles they want. |
| **Several, for good** | Once taken, a role can't be removed from here. |
| **Just one** | Taking one removes the other, clicking again removes it. For colours or region. |

In the several-roles modes, each button can depart from the menu in its options: **Second click on this button** → **Same as the menu**, **Keeps the role** or **Removes the role**. You can thus mix a notification role members can switch off with an access role they don't give back.

The text under the title follows the chosen mode: Kotbo says roles can't be removed from here, that only one may be taken, or that some can't be removed once obtained.

---

## More options

The **More options** panel of a button or of the menu sets the rest:

* the button's Discord colour (**Grey**, **Blue**, **Green** or **Red**), with no effect on granting;
* **Member count on buttons**: shows "Red · 12", the number of members who have the role (not clicks: toggling several times doesn't inflate it), updated a few seconds after a click;
* the message's **Bar color**, or the default.

## Managing published menus

Each published menu shows as a card, like a mini Discord preview, with its channel, its roles and the number of members per role.

* **Edit**: reopens the window. The Discord message is updated on save, nothing to republish.
* **Duplicate**: creates a copy of the menu, to publish elsewhere.
* **Delete from dashboard**: also deletes the Discord message.

:::caution[Changing channel republishes the panel]
If you pick another channel while editing a menu, Kotbo posts a new message in that channel and **deletes the old one**. Members lose the original message, its pins and the links pointing to it.
:::

Kotbo flags on each card what keeps the menu from working: the message is no longer on Discord, or roles it can't give (role not found, managed by an integration, placed above the bot, or the **Manage roles** permission missing). A member who clicks a blocked role gets an explanation too.

Roles taken and removed are tracked in the [Logs](/en/dashboard/moderation/logs/), the **Reaction roles** tab of [Statistics](/en/dashboard/general/analytics/) and [Triggers](/en/dashboard/communaute/declencheurs/).
