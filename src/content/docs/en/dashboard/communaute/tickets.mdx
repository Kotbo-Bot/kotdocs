<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Tickets</strong> module lets your members open a private space (channel, thread or relayed direct message) with your team. From the dashboard, staff handle them as in a help desk: queues, priorities, assignment, response targets and statistics.</p>

:::tip[On the Discord side]
To see what the panel looks like, how members open a ticket and how staff answer on Discord, read the [Discord tickets guide](/en/discord/communaute/tickets).
:::

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Tickets** section: [Open tickets ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/tickets).
The management and configuration tabs are reserved for administrators and the configured access roles.
:::

The page has seven tabs: **Tickets**, **Performance**, **Transcripts**, **Satisfaction**, **Macros**, **Blocklist** and **Configuration**.

---

## Handling tickets (Tickets tab)

### Queues

On the left, tickets are sorted into **queues**, each with its counter:

| Queue | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs reply** | Tickets where the member is waiting for a staff reply. |
| **Open** | Every open ticket. |
| **Unassigned** | Open tickets with nobody assigned. |
| **My tickets** | Those assigned to you. |
| **Overdue** | Those past a [service target](#service-targets). |
| **To approve** | Requests waiting for approval, when approval is required. |
| **Closed** and **All** | The history. |

The **Search tickets** field accepts a member, a reason, a `#tag` or an ID. Sorting switches between **Oldest first** and **Newest first**, and the `j` / `k` keys move from one ticket to the next.

In the list, each ticket shows whether it is **Waiting for a staff reply** or **Waiting on member**, its priority when it is **High** or **Urgent**, and who it is assigned to.

### The conversation

The ticket's messages show in the middle. The **Write a message...** field replies directly: the message goes to the ticket's Discord channel, under the signed-in staff account.

When [AegisAI](/en/dashboard/moderation/aegis/) analyzes ticket mood, the requester's mood shows on each ticket.

### Properties

On the right, the properties block sets:

* the **Priority**: **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**;
* **Assigned to**: a staff member (the list shows how many tickets each one already has open), or **Nobody**;
* **Tags**, to sort tickets and find them again through search;
* the **Targets**: the time left before the **First reply** and **Resolution** deadlines, or how far past them the ticket is.

Below, the member card sums up their seniority, roles, **Record** (warns, timeouts, kicks, bans), past tickets and their average rating, plus a **Staff note**. **Full case file** opens their case file without leaving the page, where a sanction can be applied; **Full profile** opens their [member profile](/en/dashboard/moderation/membres/).

### Actions

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Claim** | Assigns the ticket to you. The bot announces it in the channel. |
| **Overclaim** | Takes over a ticket already assigned to someone else, if the configuration allows it. |
| **Close** | Closes the ticket. A **Closing reason** can be added; it is optional. |
| **Reopen** | Reopens a closed ticket. |
| **Archive** / **Unarchive** | An archived ticket **keeps its channel**, which becomes read-only. |
| **Lock** / **Unlock** | Protects the ticket against deletion (see below). |
| **Rename** | Changes the Discord channel's name. |
| **Restore** | Recreates the channel of a deleted ticket, with the whole message history, original names and avatars. Three restores at most per ticket; original attachments are not restored. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the channel on Discord and generates a transcript. |

### Protecting a ticket against deletion

**Lock** opens **Protect against deletion**: while the protection holds, the ticket cannot be deleted from the dashboard nor from Discord. Pick a **Protection duration** (*7 days*, *30 days*, *90 days* or *No expiry*) and an optional **Reason**.

The ticket then shows **Protected against deletion**, with who set the lock and when it ends.

### Approving a request

With approval required, a request opens no channel until it is accepted. It shows up in the **To approve** queue:

* **Approve request** creates the ticket;
* **Reject** asks for a **Reason given to the member**, who receives the rejection by DM.

The same decision can be made from the card posted in the approval channel on Discord.

## Measuring support (Performance tab)

The tab gives support times as **medians**, with the 90th percentile to spot forgotten tickets, over the chosen **Period**. **Compare with previous period** overlays the previous period.

* The **Open now** block counts tickets without an owner and those waiting for a staff reply.
* **Per staff member**: tickets handled, median times and satisfaction for each one.
* **By type** and **By tag**: where requests pile up.
* **Ratings received**: how satisfaction ratings are spread.

## Transcripts (Transcripts tab)

This tab replaces the former **Transcripts** page, whose `/transcripts-list` address now leads here. It lists **ticket** and **channel** transcripts:

* a ticket is transcribed when it is **deleted**, and again when it is **archived**, so the archive reflects its last state;
* the `/transcript` command and importing messages as evidence into a [sanction report](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/) produce channel transcripts.

The **Type** column tells them apart: **Ticket** or `/transcript`. **View** opens the full transcript in a new tab.

Only people who manage the server settings can delete one. A confirmation comes first: the transcript disappears from the dashboard and its link stops working.

:::danger[Permanent deletion]
A deleted transcript cannot be recovered. If it serves as evidence in a sanction report, the report loses its attachment.
:::

## Tracking quality (Satisfaction tab)

When a ticket closes, the bot asks the member to rate the support they got, from 1 to 5 stars. The **Satisfaction** tab shows:

* the average rating and how ratings are spread;
* **Satisfaction per staff**;
* **Recent reviews**, with a **With comment** filter.

The comment question and reposting reviews on Discord are set in the [configuration](#satisfaction-survey).

## Macros (Macros tab)

A **macro** puts a reply the staff keeps rewriting one click away, and attaches what usually comes next.

The **New macro** button asks for:

* **Name**, **Category** and **Emoji**.
* **Content**: the text sent, 2000 characters maximum - the limit of a Discord message. The `{user}`, `{staff}`, `{ticket_id}`, `{ticket_channel}` (clickable channel mention), `{ticket_link}` (Discord link to the channel), `{ticket_type}` and `{server}` variables are replaced in it.
* **Ticket types covered** and **Allowed roles**: empty, the macro applies to every type and all staff.
* **Suggestion keywords**: macros whose keyword appears in the request move to the top of the picker.

### Attached actions

A macro can do more than write:

* **Change type to**: change the ticket's type.
* **Add the role** and **Remove the role**.
* **Trigger the satisfaction survey**.
* **Close the ticket**: applied last, since the others need a ticket that is still open.

### Automatic sending

**Send automatically on open** posts the macro as soon as the ticket is created.

:::note[Its actions do not apply]
A macro sent on opening posts its text but does not apply its actions: closing or retyping a ticket that was just born would do more harm than good.
:::

In the ticket channel, the **Macros** button opens the picker. Discord only accepts 25 entries: the most used come first.

## Blocklist (Blocklist tab)

The **Ticket blocklist** tab stops some members from opening a ticket, without going through a Discord sanction.

* **Discord ID**: the ID of the member to block.
* **Reason**: it is **shown to the member** when they try to open a ticket. Left empty, it shows as *No reason given*.
* **Duration (days)**: empty, the block is **Permanent**.
* **Allow reopening their past tickets**: the member can no longer open tickets, but can still ask to reopen one that was already handled. The entry then reads **Reopening allowed**.

Each entry shows who created it (*Added by …*). **Remove** lifts the block.

## Configuring the module (Configuration tab)

At the top of the tab, **Set up** creates what is missing - the ticket category, the panel channel and the log channel - keeping what you already picked. **Send Embed** posts the opening panel in its channel.

The settings are grouped into collapsible sections.

### Channels & roles

* **Default ticket category**, **Opening panel channel**, **Logs / transcripts channel** and **Default support staff role**.
* **Direct message relay channel (DM mode)**: where messages from tickets opened by DM arrive.
* **Allow overclaiming**: a staff member can take over an already claimed ticket; **Who can overclaim?** opens it to *All staff* or *Higher or equal grade*.
* **Auto-claim on reply**: as soon as a staff member writes in an open ticket, it is claimed by them.

A channel or role that cannot be found on the server is flagged: the module will not work while it points there.

### Embed customisation

The opening panel: **Title**, **Description**, **Button text**, **Colour**, images, author and footer. The **Interaction type** shows one button per ticket type (**Buttons**) or a single **Dropdown menu**.

### Welcome message inside the ticket

The message sent when the ticket is created, with its variables (`{type_label}`, `{user}`, `{staff_mention}`, `{reason}`, `{description}`) and its **Embed colour**.

### Approval & locking

* **Lock until claimed**: the channel stays visible to the opener and staff, but nobody can write until a staff member claims it.
* **Require approval before opening**: the request goes to staff first, and the channel is only created once approved. The **Approval channel** receives the decision cards (empty: the ticket log channel).

Each ticket type can follow these settings or override them.

### Archiving

* **Archive category**: archived channels are moved there. Empty, the channel stays put and only its permissions change.
* **The opener keeps read access**: the member keeps seeing their archived ticket without being able to write in it.

**Member-side history** adds a **"My past tickets" button** under the panel: everyone sees their closed tickets and transcripts there. **Member-side reopening** lets them ask to reopen a closed ticket (3 reopenings at most, with increasing delays) and **Member-side deletion** lets them erase one of their past tickets; locked tickets stay protected.

### Inactivity

* **Enable reminders**: an automatic message when the author stops answering, after a **Delay (hours)**, with the text of your choice.
* **Remind staff when a member is waiting**: if no staff member has answered the member after the **Delay without reply (minutes)**, the bot mentions whoever claimed the ticket, otherwise the staff role. One reminder per wait, counted in the statistics.

### Recording notice

**Show the notice before every ticket** tells the member, before their ticket is created, that the conversation will be recorded, transcribed and archived. They read it in a message only they can see, and the ticket is created once the **Reading delay** has passed (5 to 30 seconds; 10 to 15 leave time to read). The **Notice text** can be changed, and a preview shows it with its countdown.

### Service targets

Two times to keep, tracked in the ticket list and in the **Overdue** queue:

* **First reply (minutes)**;
* **Resolution (hours)**: time between opening and closing.

Empty, a time has **No target**.

### Quotas

Opening limits on the member side, and a workload cap on the staff side. Each quota is turned on separately, and a ticket type can adjust the threshold.

| Quota | What it limits |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets open at the same time** | How many tickets a member can have open at the same time. |
| **Delay between two openings** | Stops members from opening ticket after ticket without leaving time to answer. |
| **Quota over a period** | Caps openings over a rolling window (*Maximum tickets* over *N hours*). |
| **Limit reopenings** | How many times the same ticket can be reopened. The delays between two reopenings (24 h, then 7 days) apply no matter what. |

The **Maximum load per moderator** counts tickets claimed and still open; beyond it, staff are warned or refused.

### Satisfaction survey

* **Ask for a comment**: after rating, the member can add a free-text comment. The **Question asked** can be changed, and the button disappears from the DM after the **Timeout** (30 to 900 seconds). The rating is stored even without a comment.
* **Reviews channel**: every review is reposted there - rating, author, assigned staff and comment. Empty, nothing is reposted.
* **Hide the review author**: the reposted review shows *Anonymous member*. The dashboard still shows the author.

### Ticket types

Several support options ("Report a bug", "Shop question"…). **Add a type** then set:

* the **Button / option label**, the **Emoji**, the **Button colour (style)** and the **Option description (dropdown menu)**;
* the **Opening mode**: **New channel**, **Direct messages (DM)**, **Thread**, or **Default (global)**;
* the **Target category** and a **Specific staff role** (empty: the defaults);
* **Lock until claimed** and **Require approval**, specific to the type;
* **Staff anonymity** in DM mode, and creating the ticket on the [staff server](/en/dashboard/cross-serveur/serveurs-staff/) for an internal ticket (HR, complaint…).

**Enable the question form for this type** asks up to **5 questions** before opening. With no custom question, Kotbo asks *Subject* and *Description*.

| Answer type | Where the question is asked |
| --- | --- |
| **Short line** and **Long paragraph** | In the opening window (Discord modal). |
| **Dropdown menu**, **Radio buttons** and **File attachment** | In the ticket, right after it is created: the bot asks it itself. |

Choice types need their **Choices / options**, comma separated; Discord only renders the first 5 as buttons. **Required field** makes the answer mandatory.

:::caution[A question without a label blocks saving]
Discord would reject the form: Kotbo refuses to save a type with an unlabelled question.
:::
