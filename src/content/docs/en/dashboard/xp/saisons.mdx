<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Seasons</strong> add a notion of time to the leaderboard: a separate ranking starts on one date, ends on another, and makes way for the next.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Levels → Seasons** section: [Open seasons ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/seasons).
The module depends on [Leveling & XP](/en/dashboard/xp/leveling/).
:::

---

## Creating a season

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click New season</h3><p>The <strong>Create a season</strong> form opens.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Fill in the three fields</h3><p><strong>Name</strong> (for example <em>Season 1 - Summer 2026</em>), <strong>Start date</strong> and <strong>End date</strong>. All three are required.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Create</h3><p>The season appears in the list with the <strong>Upcoming</strong> status.</p></div></div>
</div>

---

## A season's life cycle

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="dash-badge">Upcoming</span> | Created but not started. The **Start** button launches it. |
| <span class="dash-badge active">Active</span> | The season leaderboard fills up. |
| <span class="dash-badge warn">Ended</span> | Closed with the **End the season** button. |
| <span class="dash-badge">Archived</span> | Kept for the record. |

---

## Following the leaderboard

* The **Current ranking** block shows the members of the running season.
* If the [Prestige](/en/dashboard/xp/prestige/) module is on, a **season RP ranking** is available too.

:::tip[RP and XP do not behave the same]
RP is reset when the season closes, unlike XP, which stays earned.
:::

The **All seasons** block lists the full history with, for each one, its number of participants.

If no season exists yet, Kotbo shows *No season created* and invites you to click **New season**.
