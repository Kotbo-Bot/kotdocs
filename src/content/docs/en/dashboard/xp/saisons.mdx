---
title: Leveling seasons
description: Rank the XP earned over a period and reward the best at closing, without ever resetting levels.
---

<p class="doc-lead">A <strong>Season</strong> ranks the XP earned between a start date and an end date, and rewards the best at closing. Levels are never reset: everyone keeps their progress, the season only counts what was earned during it.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Levels → Seasons** section: [Open seasons ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/seasons).
The module depends on [Leveling & XP](/en/dashboard/xp/leveling/).
:::

---

## Create a season

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click New season</h3><p>On a server with no season, the button reads <strong>Create my first season</strong>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Name and date it</h3><p><strong>Name</strong> (<em>Season 1 - Summer 2026</em>), start date and end date. Today's date or a past one opens the season right away, if no other is running.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Plan the announcements</h3><p>The <strong>Announcement channel</strong> gets the opening, then the podium and rewards at closing. <strong>Reminder before the end</strong> adds a message there during the last 24 hours, with the provisional leaderboard. Previews show these messages before they go out.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Add the rewards</h3><p>See <a href="#rewards">below</a>. Then create the season.</p></div></div>
</div>

**Chain automatically** opens, at closing, a season of the same length, rewards and settings, unless another one is already planned.

Only one season runs at a time: a season whose start date has passed waits for the current one to close.

---

## Rewards

They are given at closing, based on the season's XP leaderboard.

* **By rank**: **Add a reward** ties a **Rank** to a **Role** and **Coins**. Tick **Taken back at the next closing** so the role is removed at the end of the following season, unless won again.
* **First place role**: given to the season's winner and taken from the previous season's winner.
* **XP milestones**: **Add a milestone** rewards every member who earns at least **From (XP)** during the season, on top of their rank reward. Each milestone reached counts.

Coins need the [Economy](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/) module on; Kotbo warns you otherwise.

:::caution[Some rewards can't be given]
Kotbo flags ahead of time the roles Discord will refuse at closing: deleted role, managed by an integration or placed above the bot.
:::

## Current season tab

The **Seasons status** panel sums up the **Current season**, **Planned rewards**, **Announcement channel**, **Chaining**, **Next season** and **Last closed**. Advice shows up when needed: no reward planned, no Discord announcement, season ending soon.

The leaderboard shows the XP earned since the season began: a member who joined midway starts from zero. With the [Prestige](/en/dashboard/xp/prestige/) module, a season RP leaderboard is added; RP, unlike XP, is reset at closing.

* **Start now** launches an upcoming season without waiting for its date.
* Ending the season stops it before its planned end date.

At closing, the recap freezes the leaderboard, lists the **Rewards given** (or what failed: role refused by Discord, member left, Economy off, with **Retry**) and offers **Start the next season**.

---

## All seasons tab

The history, with each season's status (**Upcoming**, **Active**, **Ended**) and, for ended seasons, their **Final leaderboard**.

**Season comparison** puts the seasons side by side, from the figures frozen at each closing: **XP earned**, **Winner's XP** and **Ranked members**.

Only a season that hasn't started can be deleted.

## On Discord

Members follow the season with the commands described in [Clans, seasons and bets](/en/discord/communaute/clans/). [Triggers](/en/dashboard/communaute/declencheurs/) can react to a season opening or closing.
