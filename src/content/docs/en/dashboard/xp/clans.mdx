<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Clans</strong> module splits your community into teams based on Discord roles. Every member contributes points to their clan, and a season crowns a winner.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Levels → Clans** section: [Open clans ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/clans). The module is in **beta**.
It depends on the [Leveling & XP](/en/dashboard/xp/leveling/) module: clan points come from level-ups.
:::

---

## Clans & Roles tab

### Configuration

* **Enable clans**: turns on the `/clan` commands and the related safeguards.
* **Single Clan**: forces one clan role per member.
* **Assign a clan on server arrival**: automatically gives the least populated clan to every new member. Validated alt accounts are aligned with their main account's clan.

### End-of-season rewards

* **Season Announcement Channel**: where the winner announcement is published.
* **Giveaway Boost**: improves the winning clan's odds in giveaways.
* **Clan Leader Role**: grants the leader role to the top contributor.

### Weekly digest

The **Weekly digest** posts each Monday, in every clan's HQ, the week's points, top contributors and standing.

Kotbo names the clans without an HQ: with no channel, they will get nothing.

### Creating a clan

The **New clan** button opens a form: **Clan Name**, **Description**, **Associated Discord Role**, **Clan General (HQ)** and **Clan Leader Role**.

The **Configured clans** table then shows the **Clan**, **Discord Role**, **Clan HQ Channel**, **Leader Role**, **Members** and **Total XP** columns.

### Bulk operations

These actions run progressively in the background and require typing a confirmation word:

| Action | Word to type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Distribute** | `DISTRIBUTE` | Randomly assigns a clan to members who have none. |
| **Rebalance** | `REBALANCE` | Moves members of the most crowded clans into the clans to fill. See below. |
| **Remove from all** | `REMOVE` | Removes their clan from every member. |

:::warning[Removal recreates the roles]
Each clan role is recreated identically (channel permissions included), then the old one is deleted. **The role ID changes**: any external setting pointing at it has to be redone.
:::

:::note[Members holding several clans]
Cleaning those duplicates is no longer on this page: it runs from Discord with `/clan dedupe`, administrators only. See [Clans, seasons and bets](/en/discord/communaute/clans/).
:::

### Rebalancing into the new clans

This is the tool to use after creating a clan: without it, the new one stays empty while the old ones overflow. **Nothing changes before the final confirmation.**

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Tick the clans to fill</h3><p>Unchecked clans are the ones giving members away.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Set the target size</h3><p><strong>Target size per clan to fill</strong>, or leave it empty for the average of all clans.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Choose who leaves first</h3><p><em>Least active</em>, <em>Random</em> or <em>Most active</em>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Compute the preview</h3><p>Kotbo lists the proposed transfers, clan by clan, with the head count before and after.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Start the rebalancing</h3><p>Type <code>REBALANCE</code>. Every transfer is checked again at launch, then applied progressively.</p></div></div>
</div>

**Activity is measured as previous-season points per day of presence**, so members who joined mid-season are not penalised. Those who joined too late for that season are judged on the current one. In *Random* mode the activity shown is for information only: it decides nothing, and **New draw** gives another list.

The **Never move above** field protects the pillars: a member whose points exceed that threshold stays in their clan.

:::caution["Most active" costs the giving clans dearly]
This mode gives the new clans a strong team right away, but the giving clans lose their best players, the ones most attached to their clan. Kotbo shows a warning and points to the protection threshold.
:::

Some members are **never** moved, and the preview groups them by reason: **Clan leaders**, **Ongoing bet**, **Above threshold**, **Linked accounts in two clans**, **Several clans**. The **Keep** button sets others aside by hand - the next one on the list then takes their place.

:::note[What happens to the points]
**Current-season** points follow the member into their new clan; **previous-season** points stay with the former one.
:::

## Season Management tab

The **Current Season** block shows the season number, the **time remaining**, and the start and end dates.

* **Manually close the Season** computes the winning clan, grants the rewards, renames the HQ and resets every member's active clan XP to 0. Contribution history is kept.
* **Reset the Season** moves on to the next one.

### Planning

The **Plan the clan season** block offers quick fills (**1 month**, **Quarter**, **6 months**, **1 year**) and a **Chain after the current one** button that reuses the same duration.

**Automatic renewal** closes the season as soon as the end date passes, then immediately schedules the next one for the same duration. With no planned date, the running season will not close by itself.

## Points Management tab

### Gain per level-up

* **Points awarded per level**: the flat amount paid on each level-up.
* **Points proportional to effort**: pays according to the XP actually crossed rather than a flat amount at every level. The **Reference level** sets the balance point.

### Gain per server boost

**Points granted per boost**: the bonus paid to the clan of a member who boosts the server.

### Daily Algo points

If the [Daily Algo](/en/discord/communaute/daily-algo/) module is on, you can **convert Daily Algo points** into clan points at the end of the week, with a **conversion rate** and a **bonus**. Without this setting, the two modules run unconnected.

### Manual adjustments

* **Adjust Clan points**: adds an amount to a clan's total. The **Remove Clan points** button does the opposite.
* **Adjust Member points**: adjusts a specific member's contribution, searched by nickname, with the same add / **Remove Member points** pair.

:::note[A member in debt is repaid first]
If the member carries a clan-point debt from bets, the addition goes to repayment first: *addition recorded, but N point(s) went towards repaying the member's debt*.
:::

## Bets tab

Two members challenge each other on any subject and stake clan points; only a referee settles it. Members open a bet with the `/paris` command.

:::note[The Clans module must be on]
*The Clans module is off: bets stay inactive until it is turned back on.*
:::

### Bet formats

* **Duels with no named opponent**: duels started without naming an opponent, which anyone can take up.
* **Pool bets**: several bettors, a single winner who takes the whole pot.
* **Team bets**: sides facing off. The winning side shares the pot in proportion to what each member staked.

The **stake mode** decides the balance:

* **Per person**: everyone stakes the same amount, so an outnumbered side gets better odds.
* **Per side**: the stake is split across the side's seats, which requires declaring how many there are when the bet is created.

### Limits

**Minimum** and **maximum stake**, **Concurrent bets**, **Players per bet**, **Sides per bet** and **Reply window (h)**.

### Channels

* **Bets channel**: where `/paris` is accepted. With no setting, the command works everywhere and the bet is posted where it was started.
* **Announcements channel**: where the summary is posted once the verdict is in.

### Refereeing

The **Roles allowed to manage bets** block hands refereeing to a team without opening up server administration: naming the winner of a running bet, or cancelling it and returning the stakes. Administrators keep this right in every case.

### Betting on credit

**Betting on credit** allows a member to stake more points than they hold: the shortfall becomes a **debt**, taken from their later gains.

* **Debt ceiling**: past it, no further bet on credit is accepted.
* **Clear debts at season end**: off, a debt survives seasons; otherwise waiting for the close is enough to owe nothing.

The **Open debts** list lets you clear a member's debt. The share still staked on running bets stays owed until the verdict, unless you explicitly tick the box to clear that too.

### End-of-season rewards

When a season closes, the top bettors receive a title and a bonus. Only a positive net gain is rewarded: a season where everyone lost crowns nobody.

* **1st**, **2nd** and **3rd place bonus**, paid into the **next season**. At zero, only the title is awarded.
* **Top bettor role**: optional. It is removed from its previous holders at every closure, then given to the winner - or to all of them on a tie.

A winner in debt sees their bonus go to repayment first.

The **Latest bets** list keeps the history with the stake, the pot, the season and the status: *Pending*, *Ongoing*, *Processing*, *Settled*, *Declined*, *Withdrawn*, *Expired* or *Voided*.

## Administration tab

:::danger[Irreversible actions]
Both actions on this tab require typing a confirmation word.
:::

* **Reset all Clan data** (`RESET ALL`): permanently deletes every configured clan, the associated roles in the database, and the XP history of all seasons. To simply close the running season, use the *Season Management* tab instead.
* **Cancel the last season** (`CANCEL`): returns to the previous season. The active season's contributions are deleted, and the winner of the season before that, along with its clan leaders, is restored.

## Public page

The **Clan board** is a public page, outside the signed-in dashboard, that your members can follow live. It refreshes on its own and brings together in a single screen what used to be split between the clans page and the RPG one.

It holds:

* the season's **ranking by clan**: season XP, latest recorded scores, time remaining, with the breakdown of a clan's members;
* two tabs, **Raid** and **Adventurers**: how far each clan got on the week's boss - damage, top strikers, boss down or not - and the individual player ranking;
* the **running clan quests**, with their reset countdown;
* the **latest settled bets** (*who challenged whom, on what, and for how much*), the season's **bettor standings** and the open debts;
* a search, to find a member.

The **View the page** and **Copy the link** buttons are available from the dashboard, on the Clans page as well as on the [Economy & RPG](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/) page.

:::note[Each block has its own guard]
Clans and RPG are two separate modules: turning clans off removes the ranking, the bets and the debts, but leaves the adventurer ranking. The screen is only truly empty when both are off - *this server has enabled neither clans nor the RPG*.
:::
