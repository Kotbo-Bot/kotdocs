<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Reputation</strong> module lets your members thank each other. Unlike XP, which rewards the amount of activity, reputation rewards how useful others found someone.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Levels → Reputation** section: [Open reputation ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/reputation).
Votes are cast from Discord; the dashboard is where you read them. The `/rep` commands are covered in [Levels, leaderboards and reputation](/en/discord/xp/progression/).
:::

:::note[What the bot refuses]
A member gets **three votes a day**, counted in the server's time zone. Past that the vote is refused - and a vote that slipped through on a double submission is cancelled afterwards. Nobody can vote for themselves, nor for a bot.
:::

---

## The indicators

Three figures summarise the module's activity:

* **Total votes**: how many votes have been cast since it went live.
* **Unique voters**: how many different members have voted.
* **Total rep given**: the cumulative points handed out.

:::tip[Read the gap]
A high vote count from few unique voters usually means a handful of very active members rather than a widespread habit. That is the signal to watch to avoid mutual back-scratching.
:::

---

## Reputation ranking

The list ranks members by the reputation they received. Until anyone votes, Kotbo shows *No votes yet* and explains that member-to-member votes will appear here.

---

## Recent votes

The **Recent votes** block shows the latest exchanges as they happen: who thanked whom, and when. This is the view to check to spot unusual behaviour quickly.

When nothing has been given recently, the section shows *No recent votes*.

---

## Link with other modules

[Quests](/en/dashboard/economie/quetes/) can include a **Give +rep** objective, which encourages the habit among members who would not think of it themselves.
