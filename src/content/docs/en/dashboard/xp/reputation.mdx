<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Reputation</strong> module lets your members thank each other. Unlike XP, which rewards the amount of activity, reputation rewards how useful others found someone.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Levels → Reputation** section: [Open reputation ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/reputation).
Thanks are given from Discord, with `/rep give`, from a message's menu (**Apps → Kotbo actions → Donner de la réputation**) or by reaction if you turn it on. The `/rep` commands are covered in [Levels, leaderboards and reputation](/en/discord/xp/progression/).
:::

:::note[What the bot refuses]
Each member has a number of votes per day, counted in the server's time zone. Nobody can thank themselves or a bot.
:::

---

## Overview tab

The **Module status** panel sums up activity: **Votes per member per day**, thanks over 7 days, members who thank (7 days), **Role tiers**, active safeguards and the last thanks.

Kotbo adds advice when needed: no role tier yet (a role at 10, 50 or 100 points gives a visible goal, with a button to create one), or no thanks for a week.

The **Reputation Leaderboard** reads over a **Period**: **7 days**, **30 days** or **All time**. Clicking a member opens their profile.

---

## History tab

Every thanks, newest first: who thanked whom, when, with **Open message** when it was given from a message. Staff adjustments show up too. Clicking a member filters the history on what they gave or received; **All members** clears the filter.

The staff can **Remove this thanks** or **Undo the adjustment**: points, rewards and tier roles follow.

---

## Tiers tab

At each threshold of reputation received, the member gets the chosen role. **Add a tier** asks for the **Points** and the **Role given**. Roles stack; removing a vote removes the roles the member no longer reaches.

* **Announcement channel**: a message there announces each tier reached, with its **Announcement preview**. Without a channel, the role is given with no announcement.

:::caution[Some roles can't be given]
Kotbo warns when a role was deleted, is managed by an integration or sits above the bot's role. Move the bot's role higher in the Discord server settings. The tab then shows *Needs fixing*.
:::

## Settings tab

### Thanks settings

* **Votes per member per day**.
* **Thank with a reaction**: reacting under a message with the **Thanks emoji** gives its author a point, within the day's votes. A refusal stays silent so the channel doesn't get cluttered.

### Rewards for the thanked member

**XP per thanks** and **Coins per thanks**: what each thanks received earns. `0` for nothing.

### Anti-abuse safeguards

Optional. They limit who can thank, who can be thanked and where, so friends can't pass points back and forth and throwaway accounts can't farm them:

* **Minimum account age** and **Minimum time on the server**;
* **Wait before thanking the same member**;
* **Excluded roles** and **Excluded channels**.

A refused thanks comes with a clear message, except by reaction.

### Points management

Staff only. Changes apply right away and are written to the audit log. They give neither XP nor coins, but tier roles follow the new total.

* **Adjust**: give or remove a number of points from a member, with a reason.
* **Reset a member**: removes every point they received.
* **Reset the whole server**: deletes every thanks and adjustment. You have to type `RESET` to confirm.

:::danger[Resets are final]
Neither a member nor the server gets their points back afterwards.
:::
