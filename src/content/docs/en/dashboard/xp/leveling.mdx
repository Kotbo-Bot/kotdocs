<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Leveling & XP</strong> module rewards participation: every message and every minute spent in voice earns experience, which levels members up and grants them roles.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Levels → Levels** section: [Open leveling ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/leveling).
The module is off by default: turn it on with the **Module status** switch at the top of the page, or no XP is handed out.
:::

:::tip[On the Discord side]
What your members type to read their progression is gathered in [Levels, leaderboards and reputation](/en/discord/xp/progression/).
:::

---

## Home tab: start with a preset

The page opens on the **Home** tab and its **Pick a preset** screen. The detailed configuration stays one click away, in the tabs that follow. A preset configures XP gains and the progression curve in one go.

| Preset | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic** *(recommended)* | Steady progression that suits most servers. |
| **Fast gains** | Lots of XP and levels coming quickly, for a server that is starting out. |
| **Proportional progression** | Standard gains, but each level costs noticeably more than the previous one. |
| **Marathon** | Very slow, very spread-out progression, made for a server that has been alive for years. |
| **Prestige** | High gains but a ceiling at level 100: everyone aims for the same summit. |
| **Custom** | Your own values, tuned one by one. |

:::caution[Nothing is applied until you save]
The announcement channel and the reward roles are still set separately in the **Advanced settings**.
:::

## XP gains tab

### XP generosity

A five-step slider, from **Very low** to **Very high**, aligns the four underlying settings at once. The tiles show the result: **XP per message**, **Cooldown**, **Voice XP** and **Per hour of chatting**.

In advanced mode you set:

* **Minimum / Maximum XP per message**
* **Text XP cooldown (seconds)**
* **Voice XP per minute**

### Bonus based on message length

The longer a message, the more it earns, up to the maximum multiplier.

* **Length for the max bonus (characters)**
* **Maximum multiplier (×)**: a very short message stays at ×1, a long one reaches the chosen maximum.

### Voice XP conditions

Restricts voice XP to members who are genuinely active:

* **Require an active mic**: no XP for a muted member.
* **Require sound on**: no XP for a deafened member.
* **Ignore the AFK channel**.
* **Minimum members in voice**: XP is only granted from N connected humans. Bots do not count.

### Daily XP cap

**Maximum XP per day**, text and voice combined. `0` means no cap; the counter resets every day at midnight UTC. Manual staff adjustments are not capped.

### Exclusions and multipliers

* **Excluded channels** and **Excluded roles**: no XP is earned there.
* **XP multipliers per role**: pair a role with a multiplier. The winning clan's role is handled automatically when the clan boost is on.

## Progression tab

The curve determines the XP needed for each level, following `XP = base × level^exponent + linear × level`.

Two sliders are enough in simple mode:

* **Progression pace**: from *Very fast* to *Very slow*.
* **Gap between levels**: from *Almost none* to *Very large*.

In advanced mode: **Base coefficient**, **Linear coefficient**, **Exponent** and **Maximum level** (`0` for no ceiling; at the ceiling XP keeps accumulating but the level stops moving).

### Reading the preview

* **Preview: XP to earn per level** compares the curve being edited with the saved one.
* The **estimated time** is computed for three profiles: 10, 30 or 100 messages a day.
* **Member distribution on this curve** shows where your current members would land.
* Kotbo states how many members **will change level on save**, and how many of those go down.

:::warning[Changing the curve redistributes levels]
Levels are always recalculated from total XP. Changing the curve immediately redistributes every member's level, without costing them any XP.
:::

### Re-applying roles

After a curve change, reward roles are corrected on each member's next XP gain. The **Re-apply roles now** button applies them straight away, including for inactive members: on a large server this takes time and fills the audit log.

## Announcements tab

* **Level-up channel**: a specific channel, the **channel where the message was posted**, or a **direct message**. The **Create and save** button creates a read-only channel and selects it in one action.
* **Level-up message**: leave it empty for the default message, written in the server's language. The usable variables are listed under the field (`{user}`, `{level}`…).

### Reward roles

Pair a **Required level** with a **Role to grant**, then click **Add reward role**.

* **Stack reward roles**: members keep every role they earned instead of only the highest one.
* A reward set above the maximum level is marked **Out of reach**.

### Clan season boost

If [Clans](/en/dashboard/xp/clans/) are enabled, you can automatically grant an **XP multiplier** to the members of last season's winning clan.

## Leaderboard tab

* Statistics: **Ranked members**, **Max level**, **Average level**, **Total XP**.
* The **XP leaderboard** can be browsed and filtered by nickname.
* The **public leaderboard page** has a link to share with your members, with **View the page** and **Copy the link** buttons.

## Every member's rank card

The card shown on Discord is customised from **My profile**, at the bottom of the sidebar, in the **My rank card** section - see [My profile and preferences](/en/dashboard/general/profil/). It follows its owner across every server; the level, XP and rank shown on it stay specific to each server.

Everyone sets their own **Background**, **Background pattern**, **Avatar frame**, **XP bar**, **Username font**, up to **three emojis** and up to **three badges**. The preview updates as choices are made, and nothing is applied before **Save card**.

### Achievements

Achievements unlock **automatically**: reaching level 25, 50 or 100 on a server, topping the leaderboard of a server with at least ten members, receiving 50 reputation points, having ten messages featured on a starboard, completing 50 quests, or supporting Kotbo with a subscription. A few are **granted by hand by the Kotbo team** - bug hunter, tester, contributor, Kotbo staff - and their tile says so.

An earned achievement is shown as a **badge** on the card with one click, up to three. Some **titles**, frames, patterns and backgrounds only unlock with a specific achievement: Kotbo names which one on the locked option.

:::note[Nothing to configure server-side]
This customisation belongs to the member, not to the server. No setting on the **Leveling & XP** page affects it.
:::

## Import tab

This tab loads a list of members with their levels and XP, for example from another bot.

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Provide the JSON</h3><p>Drop a <code>.json</code> file or paste the contents directly. The expected format is an array of objects.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Analyse without writing</h3><p>The <strong>Analyse without writing</strong> option simulates the import: matched members, members with no XP yet, level changes, XP revised downwards. Nothing is modified.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Run the import</h3><p>Members that could not be found are skipped and listed with the reason.</p></div></div>
</div>

Kotbo matches each entry to a Discord member by comparing, case-insensitively, their **username**, their **display name** or their **legacy tag**.

Conversion rules:

* if only **XP** is provided, the level is derived automatically;
* if only the **level** is provided, the minimum XP for that level is granted;
* the import **overwrites** the existing levels of matched members.
