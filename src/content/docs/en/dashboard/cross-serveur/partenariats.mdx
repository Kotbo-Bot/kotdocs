## What the module tracks

A partnership is a case file, not just a line in an announcement channel. Each one carries:

| Item | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **The partner** | A Discord server, a person or an organisation. |
| **The type** | Ad exchange, permanent alliance, sister server, joint event, cross giveaway, content creator, sponsor, media outlet… twenty-five types cover the common cases, and *Custom* covers the rest. |
| **The tracking level** | **Simple**, **Tracked** or **Contractual**. Agreements and the payment schedule only show on contractual cases: a schedule on an ad swap between friends helps nobody. |
| **The stage** | The case moves from *Lead* to *Active*, then on to *Up for renewal*, *Ended*, *Paused*, *Broken off* or *Archived*. Stage jumps that would activate a partnership without validation are refused. |
| **The perks** | Partner role, channel access or a dedicated channel, showcase, pinned ad, AutoMod exemption, invite rights, XP bonus, access to giveaways… what your server grants. |
| **The commitments** | Ad posts, ad kept live, arrivals brought in, announcements relayed, prizes supplied, moderation backup… what was promised, and what gets checked. |

:::note[The module's own labels are in French]
Types, stages, perks and commitments carry French labels in both languages, on Discord as in the dashboard. They are translated here for clarity, not quoted verbatim.
:::

## What the partnership returns

Kotbo credits arrivals to the invitation of the partner who brought them, then measures what becomes of them: how many are **still here**, how many are **active**, how many **sanctions** they collected, and how many **ads** the partner actually posted. It all sums up into a health score for the partnership.

That report is what `/partnership bilan` returns.

## From Discord

Once the module is enabled, the `/partnership` command becomes available. The management subcommands require the Discord **Manage Server** permission.

| Subcommand | What it does | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/partnership liste` | Lists the running partnerships. The *etape* option shows one specific stage of the pipeline instead. | Everyone |
| `/partnership info` | Shows a partnership's file. The reply is public for the staff, visible to you alone otherwise. | Everyone |
| `/partnership bilan` | Figures: credited arrivals, retention, active members, sanctions, ads posted. | Staff |
| `/partnership demandes` | Pending partnership requests. | Staff |
| `/partnership pub` | Posts a partner's ad right now. | Staff |
| `/partnership vitrine` | Republishes the showcase of active partners. | Staff |
| `/partnership proposer` | Opens the partnership request form. | Everyone |

:::note[One command, two names]
This is the one command Discord localises: a client set to French shows it as `/partenariat`. The subcommands and options keep their French names in both languages.
:::

A server that wants to propose a partnership uses:

<DiscordCommand
  command="/partnership proposer"
  botResponse="*A dialog opens: server or project name, invitation link, member count and what you are offering.*\n\nYour request has been sent to the staff."
/>

The form asks for the server or project name, an invitation link, a member count and what is being offered. If requests are closed, Kotbo answers that this server does not take them.

:::note[Internal notes never reach Discord]
The file shown by `/partnership info` leaves the staff notes out. They stay on the dashboard.
:::

## Reacting to a partnership

Three triggers connect the module to [automations](/en/dashboard/communaute/declencheurs/):

* **Partenariat : changement d'étape** - the case moves to another stage, once the perks are applied.
* **Partenariat : engagement non tenu** - a commitment is found unmet; the *missed periods* field lets you react only on the second or third time.
* **Partenariat : arrivée attribuée** - a member arrives through a partner's invitation.

## Partner directory

The **Annuaire partenaires** page will open discovery beyond your own server: listing yourself, receiving proposals, and browsing compatible servers. It is under development as well, and a server can only be published there if it allowed listing in the module settings.
