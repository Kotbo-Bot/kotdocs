<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Custom Bot</strong> runs Kotbo under your own Discord application: your name, your avatar, your identity in your members' eyes.</p>

:::caution[Available on request]
This page is flagged **WIP** in the dashboard. The feature is available **on request**: contact the Kotbo administrator by direct message on Discord to have it enabled. It is a **paid service**.
:::

:::note[Where to find it]
The page no longer appears in the menu while the offer is not open to everyone: reach it through its address, [dash.kotbo.fr/custom-bot ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/custom-bot). The service is available on request: contact the Kotbo administrator via Discord DM to enable it (paid service).
:::

---

## What it changes

* The bot appears on your server with **your name** and **your avatar**.
* Slash commands are registered under **your Discord application**.
* Messages, embeds and panels carry your identity rather than Kotbo's.

Every feature described in this documentation stays the same: only the front changes.

---

## What it does not change

* The **command names** and their options: they stay Kotbo's.
* The **Discord permissions** each module requires.
* How the dashboard works, which stays hosted in the same place.

---

## Requesting activation

:::caution[Reserved to the CUSTOM plan]
The Custom Bot is only open to servers on the **CUSTOM plan**. A server on any other plan is refused when registering or starting a custom bot, including outside the dashboard. Only **stopping** stays available, so a server changing plan can shut down a bot already running.
:::

Until self-service configuration opens, setup goes through your Kotbo instance's administrator:

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Create your Discord application</h3><p>From the Discord developer portal, with the name and avatar you want.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Contact the Kotbo administrator</h3><p>By direct message on Discord, stating which server is concerned.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Invite the custom bot</h3><p>Once the application is configured, invite it to your server with the same permissions as Kotbo.</p></div></div>
</div>

:::danger[Never share your token]
Your Discord application's token gives full control over the bot. Send it only through the channel the administrator indicates, and regenerate it at the slightest doubt.
:::

:::note[The OAuth secret is never shown again]
The token and the OAuth secret are encrypted at rest. The secret field is not prefilled: left empty, it keeps the one already saved. If Kotbo reports an unreadable secret, type it in again.
:::
