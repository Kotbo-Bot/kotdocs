<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Channels</strong> page gathers the automations tied to your server's channels: threads opened automatically, a message always visible at the bottom of a channel, counters in voice channel names, voice channels created on demand and a trap channel against spam.</p>

:::tip[On the Discord side]
To see how automatic threads, pinned messages, counters, on-demand voice channels and the trap channel work, read the [Discord channel management guide](/en/discord/configuration/salons).
:::

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Settings → Channels** section: [Open channels ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/channels-management).
:::

The page has seven tabs: **By channel**, **Auto threads**, **Pinned message**, **Counters**, **On-demand voice**, **Access requests** and **Trap channel**.

---

## 1. By channel

The **By channel** tab, open by default, answers the question "what affects this channel?" without going through the other tabs.

It lists the server's channels; each one expands into the features that affect it: automatic threads, log exclusion, trap channel, and the games of the [Fun channels](/en/dashboard/communaute/salons-fun/) module.

:::note[A single-channel feature moves]
Some settings only point to one channel - the trap, a game. Turning them on for a channel removes them from the one that had them, and the list reloads to show it.
:::

Renaming and deleting a channel are also done from this tab.

## 2. Auto threads

Kotbo opens a thread for each new message in a channel, following the rules of a **configuration**. A channel can have several: they are tried in order, and the first one that accepts the message opens the thread.

**Add a configuration** walks through three questions:

1. **Where should threads be created?** The **Channel** and the **Configuration name**.
2. **Which messages create a thread?**
   * **All messages**, **Text**, **Links** or **Media** (images, videos, GIFs and stickers);
   * or **Custom conditions**, which combine conditions and groups: contains text, a link, media or an attachment, contains or starts with some text, matches a regular expression, minimum or maximum length, author has one of the roles or is a given member, sent by a bot, by a webhook, or is a reply. A group requires **All conditions** or **At least one condition**, and **Invert the result** takes the opposite.
3. **How should the thread be named?** **First line of the message**, **Author's name**, **"Thread by author" and start of the message**, **Author's name and date**, or **Custom rules**: a title template, the first line, the embed title, or an extract through a regular expression. Rules are tried top to bottom, and the first one that produces a title wins. A **Sample title** shows the result.

### Advanced settings

* **Thread archiving**: after 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days or 1 week without messages.
* **Who can rename the thread?**: **Moderators only**, **Moderators and author** or **Everyone**. Moderators always can; others use the `/thread rename` command, since the bot created the thread.
* **Bot and webhook messages**: evaluate them too. They can open a thread but are never deleted.
* **Evaluation order**: the configuration's place among the channel's.
* **JSON import / export**: copy or download a configuration, and import one into the draft. The export contains role and member IDs: check it before sharing it.

### Non-matching messages

**What to do with non-matching messages?** automatically deletes a message that no active configuration of the channel accepts, after a **Delay** (5 seconds to 1 hour), with an optional **Warning** (`{user}`, `{channel}`, `{delay}`). A configuration that accepts every message cannot do this.

:::note[Who is never deleted]
Members who can manage messages and bot messages are never affected. Kotbo needs the **Manage Messages** permission to delete.
:::

Each configuration can be **Duplicated**, turned off or deleted. Deleting a configuration leaves the threads already created in place.

## 3. Pinned message (stuck to the bottom of a channel)

The **Pinned message** tab keeps a message always visible at the bottom of a channel. Whenever the channel piles up the set number of messages, Kotbo deletes its previous post and sends the message again at the very bottom.

**Add a sticky message** asks for:

* **Target channel** and **Message** (it cannot be empty).
* **Display as an embed**: a coloured box, with an **Embed title** and a colour.
* **Discord JSON**: posts a full Discord payload (embeds and components) instead of plain text. An example to adapt is provided; unreadable JSON is refused.
* **Custom identity**: posts through a webhook, under the **Display name** and **Avatar** you pick, instead of the bot's identity. It needs the **Manage Webhooks** permission; the name cannot contain "discord" or "clyde".
* **Repost after (messages)** and **Minimum delay between two reposts (seconds)**, which protects against Discord's sending limits when the channel gets busy (`0` to turn it off).
* **Active**: pauses or resumes the message without deleting its configuration.

:::note[The channel can't be changed later]
A saved pinned message's channel can no longer be changed. To move it, delete it and create a new one.
:::

### Available variables

| Variable | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `{server}` or `{serveur}` | Your server's name |
| `{membres}`, `{members}` or `{memberCount}` | Total number of members |
| `{salons}` or `{channelCount}` | Number of channels |
| `{boosts}` or `{boostCount}` | The server's number of boosts |

### Available actions

* **Repost now**: forces an immediate repost without waiting for the message threshold.
* **Delete**: removes the message still shown in the channel and erases the configuration.

:::caution[Discord permissions required]
Kotbo must be able to **see the channel** and **write in it**. Without both permissions, the message is never reposted.
:::

:::note[No mention is triggered]
Mentions written in a pinned message notify nobody: the bot neutralises them when sending. An `@everyone` will not ping your server on every repost.
:::

## 4. Counters

The **Counters** tab shows server figures (members, bots, boosts…) in the name of dedicated voice channels.

### Creation category

Pick the Discord category for the statistics channels, or click **Create category** so Kotbo creates one.

### Historical statistics

:::warning[Rebuilding statistics]
**Rebuild historical statistics** rereads your channels' old messages to fill the activity charts.
* **Run the scan**: goes through the history in the background without touching the data already recorded.
* **Force re-scan**: overwrites then recalculates all the server's statistics. **This action destroys the dashboard's current history.**
:::

### Default counters

1. **Member counter**: total number of users on the server.
2. **Bot counter**: total number of bots.
3. **Role counter**: number of members with a target role (e.g. `@Staff`).
4. **Channel counter**: number of text and voice channels.
5. **Category counter**: total number of categories.
6. **Activity counter (24h)**: members who wrote or joined a voice channel in the last 24 hours.

For each counter, point to an **existing voice channel** or let Kotbo **create** it, and customise the name template (e.g. `Members: {count}`).

### Custom counters

**Add a custom counter** picks:

* the **Statistic type**: total members, bots, online members, members in voice, specific role, total channels, categories, active (24h), server boosts, or member goal;
* the **Goal (target number)**, for the *Member goal* type (e.g. `Goal: {count}/{goal}`);
* the **Name template**, where `{count}` (and `{goal}`) are replaced by the real values.

:::note[Name updates]
Discord limits renames: Kotbo updates these counters **every 10 minutes**.
:::

## 5. On-demand voice

Members create their own temporary voice channel and run it themselves with buttons. The tab is split into **Generators**, **Permissions**, **Panel** and **Active channels**.

### Generators

* **Generator voice channel**: as soon as a member joins it, Kotbo creates a temporary channel and moves them into it.
* **Creation category** and **Channel name template** (`{user}` inserts the creator's name, e.g. `{user}'s channel`).
* **Required role to use**: reserves the generator for a role. It gates access to the **generator**, not the permissions inside the created channel.
* **Additional generator channels**: other independent generators, each with its own channel, category, name template and required role.

**Channel ownership** decides what happens when the owner leaves their channel: a **Handover delay** to pick a successor, then **Who can claim the channel**: **Members present** or **Moderators only**. Staff can always claim a channel without an owner.

### Permissions of created channels

* **Default slots**: `0` for no limit. The owner can change it later.
* **Lock**: create the channel already locked. Only the owner and the roles allowed by default can join and write; staff keep access.
* **Channel text chat**: *Inherited* (follows the category), *Open* or *Closed*.
* **Roles allowed by default**: they see and join the channel without the owner having to add them, even when locked.
* **Owner powers**: what the creator gets **on their channel only** - mute, deafen, move (needed for the panel's "Kick" button), manage the channel and manage messages.

:::caution[These settings never open a closed channel]
The temporary channel stays in sync with its category: this block is added on top. If the category denies your members access, the created channel denies it too.
:::

### Panel

As soon as a channel is created, Kotbo posts a **management message** with buttons in its chat: lock or unlock, rename, limit the number of slots, kick or ban an intruder, open or close the chat.

**Panel presentation** sets how that message is shown: the **Panel mode** (V1, classic embed in columns; V2, what the bot sends today), its components, the layout and colours of the channel's state, and where the **Owner mention** goes. **Per-generator presentation** lets each generator have its own settings; turning it off erases nothing.

### Active channels

**Active temporary channels** lists open channels, with their creator, members and reservation. Administrators can **Rename** them, change the reservation and **Close channel**.

## 6. Access requests

When a temporary channel is locked or reserved, its panel shows a **Request access** button: someone outside can let the owner know without forcing their way in.

* **Who can answer**: **The owner only**, or **The owner and the staff**.
* **Notify the owner by**: **The voice channel** (a message in its chat, mentioning the owner), **Direct message** (discreet, but fails if their DMs are closed) or **A dedicated channel** for all the server's requests, picked under **Dedicated requests channel**. If the main choice fails, a fallback kicks in on its own.
* **Request expiry (minutes)**: past this delay, the button becomes available again.
* **Cooldown after a denial (minutes)**: from 1 to 1440 minutes before asking again.

The tab also holds three on-demand voice settings:

* **Admin settings**: what moderators may do on temporary channels, action by action - **Allowed** or **Admins only**. Staff roles are those from staff management.
* **Compact panel**: sub-panels are rewritten in a single message, with a Back button, instead of opening a new one for every action.
* **Reservation**: the **Reservable roles** offered by the **Reserve channel** button (empty: any role), what happens to **People already present without the role** (with a **Fallback channel**, otherwise they are disconnected), and what the button does for an owner with no reservable role: offer any role, pick members, or forbid reservation.

## 7. Trap channel

The **Trap channel** gets rid of spam bots and hacked accounts: anyone outside staff who writes in it is sanctioned on the spot.

* **Trap channel**: pick a channel or click **Create automatically**. Kotbo creates a channel with the right permissions and posts a warning message in it.
* **Sanction to apply**:
  * **Timeout** *(default)*: a 28-day writing timeout.
  * **Permanent ban**: bans the account and deletes its messages from the last 24 hours.
  * **Softban**: bans then unbans right away, which purges their recent messages.
  * **Kick**: removes the member from the server.
  * **Warning**: records a warning in their file.
* **Automatic re-invite** (with a kick): sends the kicked member a temporary invite by DM.

Save with the bar at the bottom of the screen.

:::caution[Security warning]
The trap channel is visible to every member, newcomers included, to work as a trap. Give it an explicit name and description (e.g. `#do-not-write-here`) so that a legitimate member does not get sanctioned out of curiosity.
:::
