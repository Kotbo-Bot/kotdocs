<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Channel health</strong> module analyses each channel's activity and flags the ones that are overloaded, under-used or completely dead.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Settings → Channel health** section: [Open channel health ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/channel-health). The page is in **beta**.
The module depends on **Analytics**: with no activity collection, no diagnosis is possible.
:::

---

## The four states

| State | What it signals |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="dash-badge active">Healthy</span> | The message rate and author count are consistent. |
| <span class="dash-badge warn">Overloaded</span> | Too many messages per hour: conversations trip over each other. |
| <span class="dash-badge warn">Under-used</span> | The channel exists but barely serves. |
| <span class="dash-badge kick">Dead</span> | No activity at all. |

The **Channels to ignore** field, in the *Configuration* tab, takes channels out of the analysis: they are not analysed and never raise an alert. Useful for intentionally quiet channels - honeypot, archives, announcements.

## A channel's detailed record

Clicking a channel opens the detailed view, over **7**, **30** or **90 days**.

### Summary tab

* **Messages**, **Share of the server** and rank among channels.
* **Unique authors** and average per active day.
* **Active days** and average messages per day.
* **Voice minutes** for a voice channel.
* The **activity trend** over the period, with the peak and its date.
* The **top contributors**, clickable to open the member's file.

### Activity tab

* **Activity hours**: a heatmap of messages by day and by hour (UTC), with the peak slot.
* **Daily detail** as a table.

### Members tab

The channel's contributors, ranked by volume.

### Content tab

* Counters for **attachments**, **replies** and **bot messages**.
* **Pinned messages** and **active threads**, with their message and member counts, and their locked or archived state.
* The channel's **latest messages**.

### Moderation tab

* **Deleted messages** and **edited messages**.
* **Most deleted authors** and **recent deletions**.

### Configuration tab

* **Channel settings**: category, creation date, slowmode, visibility, active threads, pinned messages, ID, type, position, NSFW, bitrate, user limit and topic.
* **Permissions per role**.
* **Change history**: the channel's and its permissions' changes, with their author.

---

## What is missing without logging

Some views say *Enable message logging to see this data*. They depend on the index of the [Message search](/en/dashboard/moderation/recherche-messages/) page.

:::note[Deleted channel]
If a channel no longer exists on Discord, Kotbo says so and shows only its statistical history.
:::

---

## From Discord

The `/channelhealth` command covers the essentials of the page: `analyse` runs an analysis, `activer` and `desactiver` drive the automatic watch, `config` sets its thresholds, and `archiver` files away an inactive channel.

:::caution[Administrators only]
Discord hides the command, **archiving included**, from anyone without the **Administrator** permission on the server.
:::
