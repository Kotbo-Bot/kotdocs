<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Outgoing webhooks</strong> send your server's events - joins, sanctions, tickets, suggestions… - in real time to the address of your choice. Every delivery is signed, retried on failure and recorded in a log.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Integrations → Outgoing webhooks** section: [Open outgoing webhooks ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/webhooks).
The **Integrations** group only shows for server administrators. The page carries the **Beta** badge.
:::

---

## Creating a webhook

Click **Add endpoint**:

* **Name**: to find it again, for example the tool's name.
* **Endpoint URL**: a **public HTTPS** address. Internal addresses are refused.
* **Events to send**: tick the ones you want, or **All events**, which includes events Kotbo adds later.

On creation, Kotbo shows the **signing secret**. Copy it and store it in your tool: you can reveal it again, but every reveal is logged.

A server can have up to **10 webhooks**.

### Available events

| Category | Events |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | Join, join with an identified invite, leave, roles updated, level up. |
| **Moderation** | Sanction applied, sanction revoked, auto-moderation rule triggered (without the caught text). |
| **Tickets** | Ticket opened, closed, rated by the member. |
| **Community** | Form response, suggestion proposed or resolved, giveaway winner and end, partnership stage change. |
| **Server structure** | Channel or role created, channel or role deleted. |

:::note[What is never sent]
Message content never goes out in a webhook.
:::

## Following a webhook

The list shows each endpoint, **Active** or **Paused**. Click one to open its card:

* **Send test**: sends a test event, to check that your tool answers.
* **Success rate (24 h)**, **Last delivery** and last response received.
* **Signing secret**: **Reveal** or **Regenerate**. After regenerating, update your tool, or it will reject deliveries.
* **Subscribed events**.
* **Delivery log**: every attempt, with request and response, kept for **30 days**. A delivery opens in detail (**Status**, **HTTP code**, **Attempts**, **Duration**, **Next retry**, **Request body**, **Response received**) and can be **Redelivered**.

**Edit** changes the name, the address or the events, and **Delete** removes the webhook.

---

## Retries and automatic shutdown

A delivery succeeds when your service answers with a **2xx** code within 10 seconds. Redirects are not followed.

Otherwise, Kotbo retries it after 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h, 12 h and 24 h: **8 attempts** in total. The count of consecutive failures shows on the card.

:::caution[A webhook can switch itself off]
After **40 failures in a row**, the webhook disables itself. The card shows *This webhook was disabled*: check that your service answers 2xx, then click **Re-enable**.
:::

The same event may arrive twice: deduplicate it with its ID (the `Kotbo-Event-Id` header).

## Verifying the signature

Every request is a JSON `POST` signed with HMAC-SHA256 using the webhook's secret. Four headers come with it:

| Header | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `Kotbo-Signature` | `t=timestamp,v1=signature`, the signature covering `timestamp.body`. |
| `Kotbo-Event` | The event name, for example `member.joined`. |
| `Kotbo-Event-Id` | The event's stable ID, to deduplicate. |
| `Kotbo-Delivery` | The ID of this attempt. |

The page's **Verify the signature** block gives a ready-to-copy verification example with **Copy example**, to hand to whoever connects your tool.
