<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Hierarchy</strong> describes how your team is organised: several possible branches (moderation, events, technical…), each with its ordered grades and its associated Discord roles.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Staff roles** section: [Open the hierarchy ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/staff-management/roles).
The module depends on the [Staff directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/).
:::

---

## Creating a hierarchy

The **New Hierarchy** button opens a form:

* **Hierarchy name**: for example *Moderation*, *Events*.
* **Description**: what this branch is for.
* **Color (Hex)** and **Emoji / Icon**, to tell it apart visually.
* **Responsible Discord Role** and **Responsible Member**.
* **Parent hierarchy**: lets you nest branches and build the org chart.

:::caution[Deleting a hierarchy]
The roles attached to it are not deleted: they are **detached** and move to the *Roles Outside Hierarchy* list.
:::

---

## Creating a staff role

The **New Role** button pairs a staff grade with a Discord role.

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Pick the Discord role</h3><p>The search field filters the server's roles. You can also create a <strong>custom role</strong> with no Discord counterpart.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Attach it to a hierarchy</h3><p>Or leave it outside if it is global.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Give it a weight</h3><p>The <strong>Role weight / level</strong> decides its place in the order. That is how Kotbo knows what a promotion is.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Mark the chief if needed</h3><p>The <strong>Hierarchy chief</strong> option adds the <strong>Chief</strong> badge and pushes the role to the top of the org chart.</p></div></div>
</div>

## Ordering the roles

At the top of the tab, the **Promotion ladder** lines grades up from left to right: **Promote** moves one step to the right. Each hierarchy then has its own card, with its grades and their holders.

Roles can be rearranged by drag and drop within their hierarchy. Kotbo saves the order as you go and says so with a *Saving order…* message.

Each role card recalls its **weight**, the **linked Discord role** (or the *Custom role* label) and offers the **Edit** and **Delete** actions.

:::note[Roles outside hierarchy]
The **Roles Outside Hierarchy** block collects global or uncategorised roles. When it reads *All roles are attached to a hierarchy*, your structure is complete.
:::

:::caution[What a deletion affects]
Before deleting a grade or a hierarchy, Kotbo spells out what it affects: members holding the grade, the hierarchy's grades and members, and attached sub-hierarchies.
:::

---

## What the hierarchy changes elsewhere

* **Promotions and demotions** in the [Directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/) follow the weight order.
* **Staff polls** weight votes by grade.
* **Tutoring checklists** can target a specific hierarchy and grade.
* **Staff server synchronisation** relies on these roles.
