<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Tutoring</strong> guides a new staff member until they are validated: a tutor, objectives to acquire, missions, a logbook and regular check-ins. Kotbo handles the follow-up and the reminders.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
Section **Team → Tutoring**: [Open tutoring ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/tutoring).
The module depends on the [Staff directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/). Tutor status is given from a staff member's profile.
:::

---

## What everyone sees

The page adapts to whoever opens it:

* **My tutoring**: yours, if you are being tutored.
* **My tutees**: those you follow as a tutor, with overdue check-ins.
* **All tutorings**: for administrators and anyone allowed to manage tutoring. **Ongoing** and **Finished** filters, and search by name or grade.

Each tutoring shows as a card: the target grade, the tutor, the objectives acquired and the current day out of the planned length.

## A tutoring's page

Clicking a card opens the tutoring's page.

* **Objectives**: those of the programme that apply to this tutoring. Each one is ticked **Seen** (explained or shown) then **Acquired** (done alone, without help).
* **Missions**: **Give** creates a mission in the missions list, assigned to the tutee. Opening the list leads to the full board. Without a list, **Create the missions list** sets it up.
* **Logbook**: kept by the tutee, who writes down what they did, learnt or what is blocking them. The tutor reads it before each check-in.
* **Check-ins**: the history of reviews, with their rhythm (*Every 7 d*).
* **Presence**: time spent in voice and upcoming absences.

### Add a check-in

**Add a check-in** asks for a **Feeling** (*Going well*, *To watch* or *Worrying*) and the **Check-in** itself: what works, what still needs work, what comes next. The tutee reads it. Then **Save check-in**.

Kotbo reminds tutors every day when a check-in is coming up or overdue.

### Conclude

**Conclude** shows where the tutoring stands (days elapsed against the minimum, objectives acquired, number of check-ins), then asks for the **Decision**:

* **Validate**: the tutoring ends and the person is told they are validated.
* **Do not validate**: the tutoring ends without validation; the person is told a manager will get back to them.

The **Closing note** stays on the page. Before the minimum length, only an administrator can validate, with **Validate anyway**.

:::caution[Deleting a tutoring]
Ticked objectives, check-ins and the logbook are erased. The missions list stays.
:::

## The programme

The **Programme** button opens the **Tutoring programme**: the objectives to acquire, grouped by stage, and the tutoring rhythm.

* **Add an objective**: an **Objective** (*Know how to apply a sanction with the bot*), **Details**, a **Stage** to group them (*Week 1*, *Tools*, *Moderation*…) and, if needed, the target grade it applies to. Without a grade, the objective applies to every tutoring.
* **Rhythm**:
  * **Minimum length (days)**: before that, only an administrator can validate.
  * **Check-in every (days)**: counted from the last check-in.
  * **Tutor reminder (days before)**: `0` sends none.

Deleting an objective removes it from the programme and from every tutoring, along with what was ticked.
