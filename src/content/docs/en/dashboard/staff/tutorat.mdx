<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Tutoring</strong> module supervises a new staff member's trial period: a tutor, a checklist of skills, regular reports and a final validation.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Tutoring** section: [Open tutoring ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/tutoring).
The module depends on the [Staff directory](/en/dashboard/staff/annuaire/). Tutor status is granted from a member's record.
:::

---

## Creating a tutoring track

<div class="step-list">

  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Pick the apprentice</h3><p>A staff member in their trial period.</p></div></div>

A track can also be created automatically when a member is added to the directory, or after a successful oral interview in the [Recruitment](/en/dashboard/staff/recrutement/) module.

## Tutor Dashboard tab

This is the tutor's view of their apprentices. For each one:

* their **progress** through the checklist;
* the date of the **next report**: *In N days* or *To do as soon as possible*;
* their **voice activity** and session count;
* their **upcoming absences**.

The **Handover Checklist** lists the skills, each moving from **Known** to **Acquired**. A validated item shows its date and the validator's name.

### Writing a report

The **Write a Report** button publishes a progress note on the apprentice, with a **Positive**, **Neutral** or **Negative** tone.

### Concluding

* **Validate** closes the track successfully. Kotbo compares the **current duration** with the **required duration**; if the minimum time is not met, a link lets you **force the validation**.
* **Fail** marks the track as unsuccessful.

In both cases, a **Final notes** field lets you leave a comment on the period.

---

## My Progress tab

This is the apprentice's view:

* their progress and their assigned tutor, with a **Contact on Discord** shortcut;
* their **voice activity** and their **absences**;
* the **latest feedback** published by their tutor;
* a **Logbook** where they record what they learn as they go.

When nobody is in tutoring, the page shows *You are not currently in a tutoring period*.

---

## Configuration tab

### Settings

* **Report interval (days)**: how often progress notes are expected.
* **Reminders before the deadline (days)**.
* **Minimum trial time (days)**: the duration below which validation must be forced.
* **Voice activity**: shows or hides the voice statistics.
* **Absences**: shows or hides absences.
* **DM notifications**: automatic reminders sent to tutors.

### Training checklist

The **New Item** button creates an objective:

* **Objective title**: for example *Using the admin console*.
* **Category**: *Tools*, *Knowledge*, *Access*, or your own.
* **Detailed description** shown to both mentor and apprentice.
* **Target hierarchy** and **Target grade**: leave on *Common* for the item to apply everywhere.

:::tip[One checklist per branch]
Targeting the hierarchy avoids asking an event host for a moderator's skills. Keep only what the whole staff must know as *Common*.
:::
