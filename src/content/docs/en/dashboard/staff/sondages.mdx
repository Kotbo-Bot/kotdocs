<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Staff polls</strong> are for internal decisions: every team member votes, and their vote weighs according to their grade in the hierarchy.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Staff members → Polls** section: [Open the polls ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/staff-management/polls).
Vote weights come from the [Staff hierarchy & roles](/en/dashboard/staff/hierarchie/).
:::

---

## Creating a poll

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click New Poll</h3><p>The form opens.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Write the question</h3><p><strong>Poll title</strong> (for example <em>New rules for the general channel</em>) and <strong>Description</strong> covering the subject of the vote.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Add the options</h3><p>The <strong>Add an option</strong> button creates as many as needed. At least two valid options are required.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Set an automatic close (optional)</h3><p>Then click <strong>Publish the poll</strong>.</p></div></div>
</div>

---

## Following the results

Each poll shows:

* its status: <span class="dash-badge active">Ongoing</span> or <span class="dash-badge">Closed</span>;
* its author and its end date;
* the total expressed in **vote points**, not in number of voters.

:::tip[Why points rather than votes]
Voting is weighted: each grade carries a weight from the hierarchy. A poll can therefore lean one way even when the head count is balanced. The applied weight is shown when hovering each vote.
:::

---

## Closing

The **Close now** button ends the poll before its deadline. Kotbo asks for confirmation, then freezes the results.

When no vote is running, the section shows *No active poll*.
