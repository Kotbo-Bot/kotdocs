<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Directory</strong> is your server's team sheet: who is on staff, at what grade, since when, and how active they are.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Staff members** section: [Open the directory ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/staff-management/members).
Access is controlled by the **Permissions** tab on the same page.
:::

Four key figures open the page, and each leads to the matching tab: **Team** (with tutors and suspended members), **Active warnings** (and how many members are close to the threshold), **Activity drop** this week and **Ongoing polls** (and the ones you have not voted in). The tabs carry their counters too.

---

## Members tab

The directory is a table: member, grade, **Hierarchies**, **Warnings** (with a gauge that fills up towards the demotion threshold), activity and **Actions**.

* **Search a staff member** filters by name.
* The filter switches between **All**, **Active**, **Suspended**, **Tutors** and **Warned**, with their counters.
* **Sort by** **Grade**, **Name**, **Tenure** or **Activity**.

### Importing from Discord

The **Import** button adds every holder of a Discord role to a hierarchy at once, at a chosen target grade. Kotbo then shows how many members were scanned, imported and skipped (already there).

### Acting on a member

**Promote** and **Demote** always stay visible on the row; Kotbo warns when the maximum or minimum grade is reached. The **…** menu holds the rest:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Warn** | An internal warning, which counts towards automatic demotion. |
| **Designate as Tutor** / **Remove tutor status** | Makes the member eligible to supervise an apprentice. |
| **Manage hierarchies** | Links the member to secondary hierarchies, each with its own grade. |
| **Suspend** / **Reinstate** | Keeps the grade and history, but cuts staff permissions. Reversible at any time. |
| **Blacklist** | Removes from staff and blocks any return (see [Discipline](/en/dashboard/staff/discipline/)). |
| **Dismiss** | Removes the member from staff. |

Before dismissing a member, Kotbo spells out what the removal affects: their grade and rankings.

:::tip[Suspend rather than dismiss]
Suspension is meant for long absences or situations under review: the member keeps their whole history and gets their permissions back when reinstated.
:::

### A staff member's case file

Clicking a member opens their usual case file, with an extra **Staff** tab: when they joined the staff and their current grade, **At a glance** (messages, voice, sanctions issued, progression score, 7-day pace, tenure), **Hierarchies**, **History** and **Discipline**, which recalls the demotion and blacklist thresholds. The **Promote**, **Demote** and **Warn** buttons are there too.

## Org Chart tab

The org chart draws the hierarchies and their leads. Each block shows the **Global Staff Head**, the hierarchy **Heads** and the role **Leads**.

Until a hierarchy exists, Kotbo invites you to create one and assign roles to it.

---

## Leadership tab

This view follows the team's activity.

* **Activity drop this week** lists at the top the members whose pace is falling.
* **Activity Comparison**: each member's messages per day, the month's average in grey and the week's in colour.
* The **Average score**, **Week vs month**, **Members tracked** and **Declining** figures.
* **Activity per member**: score, **30d Average**, **7d Average** and status, sortable by **Alerts first**, **Score** or **Activity**. A member whose activity collapses is marked <span class="dash-badge kick">INACTIVITY DETECTED</span>; the others are **Steady**.

Until the team has a few days of activity, the tab says so.

---

## Permissions tab

Two settings control who sees what:

* **Staff Directory**: access to the list of staff members.
* **Hierarchy & Roles**: access to managing staff roles and hierarchy.

---

## Global configuration

The **Global Configuration** block on the *Staff roles* tab carries the team-wide settings:

* **Base Staff Role** and **Trial Staff Role**.
* **Meeting announcement channel** and **Meeting voice / conference channel**.
* **Staff announcements channel**.
