<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Directory</strong> is your server's team sheet: who is on staff, at what grade, since when, and how active they are.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Staff members** section: [Open the directory ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/staff-management/members).
Access is controlled by the **Permissions** tab on the same page.
:::

Four indicators open the page: **Members** on the team, configured **Staff Roles**, active **Warnings** and **Active Polls**.

### Importing from Discord

The **Import** button adds every holder of a Discord role into a hierarchy at once, at a chosen destination grade. Kotbo then reports how many members were analysed, imported and skipped (already present).

### Acting on a record

Each card shows the member, their grade, their start date on the team, their warning count and their activity (messages, minutes, sanctions).

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Promote** / **Demote** | Moves the grade by one step. Kotbo warns when the minimum or maximum grade is reached. |
| **Designate as Tutor** | Adds the **Tutor** badge and makes the member eligible to supervise an apprentice. |
| **Manage hierarchies** | Links the member to secondary hierarchies, each with its own grade. |
| **Suspend** | Removes their Discord staff roles, but keeps their record, grade and history. Reversible at any time. |
| **Dismiss** | Removes the member from the staff. |

:::tip[Suspend rather than dismiss]
Suspension is made for long absences or situations still under review: the member keeps their whole history and gets their roles back on reinstatement.
:::

## Org Chart tab

The org chart draws the hierarchies and their leads. Each block shows the **Global Staff Head**, the hierarchy **Heads** and the role **Leads**.

Until a hierarchy exists, Kotbo invites you to create one and assign roles to it.

---

## Leadership tab

This view analyses the team's performance.

* **Score distribution** and **Activity comparison** as charts.
* The table shows the **Progression Score**, the **Activity (30d / 7d)** in messages per day, and the **Status / Alerts**.
* A member whose activity collapses is flagged <span class="dash-badge kick">INACTIVITY DETECTED</span>; the others carry the <span class="dash-badge active">ACTIVE</span> badge.

---

## Permissions tab

Two settings control who sees what:

* **Staff Directory**: access to the list of staff members.
* **Hierarchy & Roles**: access to managing staff roles and hierarchy.

---

## Global configuration

The **Global Configuration** block on the *Staff Roles* tab carries the team-wide settings:

* **Base Staff Role** and **Trial Staff Role**.
* **Meeting announcement channel** and **Meeting voice / conference channel**.
* **Staff announcements channel**.
