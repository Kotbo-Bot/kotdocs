<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Planning</strong> gathers meetings, Discord calls, absences and tasks for your team on a single agenda.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Schedule** section: [Open the planning ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/planning).
You must be registered as a staff member to create anything here.
:::

:::note[Times follow the server's zone]
Every date entered on this page - absences, calls, tasks - is read in the **server's time zone**, recalled below the fields as *Time interpreted in …* and set from the [dashboard home](/en/dashboard/general/accueil/).
:::

---

## Reading the agenda

Four calendars overlay each other, each toggled separately under **My calendars**:

| Calendar | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Meetings** | Team check-ins, covered [below](#meetings). |
| **Discord calls** | Scheduled voice calls. |
| **Absences** | Declared unavailability. |
| **Tasks** | Assigned tasks. |

The **People** panel filters the agenda on one or more members, with **None** and **All** shortcuts.

The **My day** view shows your deadlines for today and, when there is nothing, the message *Your day is free!*.

## Meetings

Meetings no longer have a page of their own: you create and follow them here. The old `/meetings` address opens the Schedule.

### Planning a meeting

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Open New event</h3><p>Pick the <strong>Meeting</strong> type at the top of the window.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Fill in the title and the time slot</h3><p>The title is required, the end must come after the start, and a meeting already in the past is refused.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Write the agenda</h3><p>The description is posted with the announcement.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Save</h3><p>Kotbo announces the event on Discord and turns on the reply buttons.</p></div></div>
</div>

Under the dates, Kotbo shows which time zone the time is read in. For a meeting, the **Change** button lets you enter it in another time zone, for that meeting only.

The announcement channel and the voice channel for meetings are set in [Server settings](/en/dashboard/configuration/centre-gestion/), **Channels and roles** tab: the **Meeting Announcements** and **Meeting Voice** fields.

### Following a meeting

Click a meeting on the agenda to open its card. It goes through the **Scheduled**, **In progress**, **Completed** and **Cancelled** statuses:

* **Start** moves it to in progress, **End** closes it;
* the **Attendance list** counts who is **Present**, **Excused** and **Absent**. Attendance is taken in the voice channel, and an absence declared over the slot excuses the member automatically.

### Deleting a meeting

Deletion is permanent. Kotbo then asks whether to clean up Discord too: **Delete the Discord event**, **Delete the announcement message** and **Delete internal notifications**.

## Declaring an absence

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Create an Absence item</h3><p>From <strong>New event</strong>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Fill in the reason</h3><p>The <strong>absence reason</strong> is required. The type is picked among <em>Holidays / Leave</em>, <em>Illness / Medical</em>, <em>Exams / Studies</em> or <em>Other reason</em>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Pick the superior to notify</h3><p>This field is required: they are the one who will receive the request.</p></div></div>
</div>

An absence is <span class="dash-badge warn">Pending</span> until approval, then <span class="dash-badge active">Approved</span>.

### Absence notifications

The alert configuration can enable a **Discord relay**: a message is sent to a chosen **alerts channel**, with a **role to mention**, and optionally through a **Discord webhook URL**.

## Scheduling a call

A call creates a voice channel or a stage at a given time.

* **Temporary channel** or **Existing channel**.
* **Voice** or **Stage** type.
* **Auto-delete when empty**: cleans up the temporary channel after the call.
* **Guests - Staff** and **Guests - Server members**: the search covers the server's members.

### Who can create calls

The **Call permissions** button opens the setting:

* **Everyone**: all members with access to the staff dashboard.
* **Specific roles / members**: a list of **allowed roles** and **allowed members**.

## Tasks and reminders

A **task** carries a title, a description, a **priority** (*low*, *medium*, *high*) and an assignee. Tasks marked **Important** move to the top.

On any agenda item, the **Reminders** block schedules a notification:

* a **date and time**;
* a **delivery channel**: *Direct Message* or *This Discord channel*;
* a free-text **note**, for example *Meeting in 5 min*.

Each reminder then shows whether it is *(Scheduled)* or *(Sent)*, and can be deleted.
