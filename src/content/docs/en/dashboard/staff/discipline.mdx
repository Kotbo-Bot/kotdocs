<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Discipline</strong> handles misconduct inside the team: internal warnings, blacklist, and automatic sanctions triggered by accumulation.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Team → Staff members → Warnings** section: [Open discipline ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/staff-management/warnings).
Not to be confused with [Sanctions](/en/dashboard/moderation/sanctions/), which cover server members.
:::

## Internal warnings

### Issuing a warning

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click New warning</h3><p>The form opens.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Point at the target member</h3><p>The search covers staff members; the Discord ID fills in automatically.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Describe the misconduct</h3><p>The <strong>Reason for the warning</strong> field expects a precise description of the facts.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Set an expiry date</h3><p>Then click <strong>Sanction</strong>.</p></div></div>
</div>

### Following warnings

The table shows the **Member**, **Reason**, **Issued by**, **Date**, **Status** and **Actions** columns. A warning stays <span class="dash-badge warn">Active</span> until its expiry date, then turns <span class="dash-badge">Expired</span>.

## Automation & sanctions

The configuration button at the top of the page opens **Automation & Sanctions**, which defines what happens when a member accumulates warnings.

At a given threshold (in number of warns), you can trigger:

* **Remove roles (Demotion)**: strips every staff role.
* **Permanent Blacklist**: the member is banned from staff by default.

:::tip[Set the threshold before you need it]
Deciding the rule while calm avoids having to judge case by case under pressure, and makes the decision defensible to the rest of the team.
:::

## Staff blacklist

The blacklist cuts off a user's access to the team entirely: they can no longer be added to staff or apply.

1. Click **Blacklist**.
2. Point at the member to blacklist.
3. Fill in the **Reason for exclusion**.
4. Set a **Blacklist end date**, or leave it empty for a **permanent** exclusion.
5. Click **Apply Blacklist**.

Each entry carries the <span class="dash-badge kick">Blacklisted</span> badge, its reason and its end date (or the **Permanent** label). The **Remove blacklist** button lifts the exclusion.

When nobody is blacklisted, Kotbo shows *No user is currently on the staff blacklist*.

---

## Link with recruitment

A blacklisted user cannot be onboarded through the [Recruitment](/en/dashboard/staff/recrutement/) module. A failed oral interview separately applies a one-month wait before any new application.
