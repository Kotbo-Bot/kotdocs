title: Doing lists
description: Trello-style boards to follow staff projects, with stages, assigned cards and pinned suggestions or tickets.

<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Doing lists</strong> are boards split into stages: one card per task, dragged from one column to the next, assigned to a staff member and then added to their schedule. Suggestions, tickets and schedule tasks can be pinned to them.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
Section **Team → Doing lists**: [Open doing lists ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/doing-lists).
The page is for the server's staff. Creating and moving cards requires the right to moderate or configure the module; deleting a list requires the delete right.
:::

---

## Create a list

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Click New list</h3><p>On a server with no list yet, the button reads <strong>Create my first list</strong>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Name it</h3><p><strong>Name</strong> (for example <em>Shop redesign</em>) and, if needed, a <strong>Description</strong>.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Pick its key</h3><p>The <strong>Key</strong> prefixes every card reference: <code>BOUT-12</code>. Left empty, it is taken from the name. It has 2 to 10 letters or digits and starts with a letter.</p></div></div>
  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Link modules (optional)</h3><p>Under <strong>Linked modules</strong>, tick <strong>Suggestions</strong> or <strong>Tickets</strong>: their items can then be pinned to the list from their own page. Then <strong>Create list</strong>.</p></div></div>
</div>

The list starts with three stages: **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**. The index shows each list as a card, with its progress.

:::tip[Changing the key later]
Changing the key from **Edit list** renames every reference in the list: `BOUT-12` becomes `SHOP-12` everywhere.
:::

## The board

Each list has its own page. **Add a card** at the top of a stage creates a free card; you then drag it from stage to stage with the mouse, or with your finger on a phone.

* **Filter cards…** searches titles, references, labels and the assignee's name.
* **My cards** keeps only the ones assigned to you.

While a filter is on, cards can no longer be dragged: clear it to rearrange the board.

### Stages

**Add a stage** creates a new one (*In review*, *Blocked*, *To test*…). A stage's menu lets you rename it, **Move left** or **right**, or delete it: its cards move to another stage, they are not erased.

* **Card limit**: a full stage refuses new cards. Left empty, the stage has no limit.
* **Final stage**: a card dropped here is done, and so is its linked schedule task.

The small number at the top of a stage adds up the story points of its cards.

## A card's details

Clicking a card opens its details. Everything is saved as you go, with no button to press.

| Field | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** and **Stage** | What the card is about, and where it stands. |
| **Assigned to** | The staff member in charge. The card then shows up in their [schedule](/en/dashboard/staff/planning/) tasks. |
| **Priority** | **No priority**, **Low**, **Medium** or **High**. |
| **Due date** | The expected date. |
| **Labels** | Free words (*bug*, *shop*, *urgent*), ten at most. Press Enter to add. |
| **Description** and **Checklist** | The context, then the points to tick. |

**More fields** unfolds the rest:

* **Type**: **Task**, **Bug**, **Story** or **Epic**.
* **Estimate**: in points.
* **Parent card**, and the list of its **Sub-tasks**.
* **Linked cards**: a card **blocks**, **is blocked by**, **relates to**, **duplicates** or **is duplicated by** another card, found by its reference (`BOUT-12`) or its title. A blocked card carries the **Blocked** badge on the board.

At the bottom, **Activity** traces the history (creation, stage changes, assignment, edits, links) and the **Comments** thread.

:::caution[Removing a card]
Removing a free card from the list deletes it with its details. An assigned card leaves its task in the person's schedule.
:::

## Pin a suggestion, a ticket or a task

From the [Suggestions](/en/dashboard/communaute/suggestions/) page, [Tickets](/en/dashboard/communaute/tickets/) or a [Schedule](/en/dashboard/staff/planning/) task, the **Doing lists** button offers the lists linked to that module: **Add to this list**. The item becomes a card that points back to its original (**Open in Suggestions**…).

Removing that item from the list leaves the original untouched. So does deleting a whole list: only its cards go away.

## Create a card from Discord

On a message, right-click then **Apps → Kotbo actions → Créer une carte de suivi** turns it into a card. The window asks for the **list key** (empty: the server's first list) and a **title** (empty: the start of the message).

The action is for the server's staff, and needs the Doing lists module to be on.

:::note[With tutoring]
A [tutoring](/en/dashboard/staff/tutorat/) can carry its own list of missions: they are the same boards.
:::
