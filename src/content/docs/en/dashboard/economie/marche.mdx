<p class="doc-lead">The <strong>Marketplace</strong> is your server's auction house: members list their items for sale, at a fixed price or by auction. The dashboard is where you supervise listings and transaction history.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Economy & games → Marketplace** section: [Open the marketplace ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/marketplace).
The module depends on [Economy & RPG](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/): with no currency and no items, it has nothing to trade.
:::

---

## The indicators

* **Active listings**: how many sales are running.
* **Total transactions**: how many sales have completed.
* **Total volume (coins)**: the currency that changed hands.

:::tip[Reading the volume]
Volume collapsing while listings stay numerous usually means prices are too high for the amount of currency in circulation. Adjust the gains in [Economy & RPG](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/) rather than the marketplace itself.
:::

---

## Listings tab

The list shows the running sales, each with:

* its **type**: <span class="dash-badge">Fixed price</span> or <span class="dash-badge active">Auction</span>;
* its **price in coins**, or the **current bid** for an auction;
* its **time remaining**, in days or hours, or the **Expired** label.

A listing's status moves between **For sale**, **Sold**, **Cancelled** and **Expired**.

When no sale is running, Kotbo reminds you that players create their listings with the `/market` command.

---

## History tab

The history keeps completed transactions. This is the view to check to verify a disputed trade or to spot an item circulating suspiciously fast between two accounts.

Until a sale happens, the section shows *No recent transactions*.
