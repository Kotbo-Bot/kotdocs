<p class="doc-lead">A <strong>pace</strong> sets the daily rewards, their cooldown and energy in one go. It is the shortest path to starting an economy without configuring anything in detail.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **General → Bien démarrer** page, **Configurations rapides** block, **Économie** card: [Open the quick setup ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/economy-setup). It no longer has a menu entry of its own.
The page follows the state of the **Economy** module: turned off, it stays greyed out, with the switch just above to turn it back on.
:::

---

## Choosing a pace

Click the card that suits you, then save. **Nothing is applied until you save.**

| Pace | Daily reward | Cooldown | Energy | Who for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Steady** | 25 to 75 | 24 h | 60, +5 / h | Modest rewards: the currency keeps its value over time. |
| **Standard** *(recommended)* | 50 to 150 | 20 h | 100, +10 / h | The recommended values: a daily slightly more frequent than once a day, regular adventures. |
| **Generous** | 100 to 250 | 12 h | 150, +20 / h | A server starting out or wanting momentum, with back-to-back adventures. |
| **Custom** | your values | - | - | Your own values, set one by one in the detailed configuration. |

Every card shows its tiles: **Daily reward**, **Daily cooldown**, **Per day** and **Energy**. The card of the pace already saved is marked **Active**; the one you just clicked, **Selected**.

The adventure cooldown follows the pace too: one adventure every 60, 30 or 15 minutes depending on the card chosen.

---

## What a pace leaves alone

A pace only changes six values. The **currency name**, its **emoji**, and the **RPG**, **shop** and **guild** modules stay as they are.

For everything else - anti-abuse limits, items, bestiary, raid, quests, black market - go through **Detailed configuration**, which opens the [Economy & RPG](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/) page.
