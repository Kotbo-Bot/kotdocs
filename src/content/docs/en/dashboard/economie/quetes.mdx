<p class="doc-lead"><strong>Quests</strong> give your members a reason to come back every day: a concrete objective, and a reward in currency and XP.</p>

:::note[Where to find it]
The **Economy & games → Quests** section: [Open quests ↗](https://dash.kotbo.fr/quests).
Coin rewards assume the [Economy & RPG](/en/dashboard/economie/economie/) module is on.
:::

---

## The indicators

* **Configured quests**: how many quests have been created in total.
* **Active quests**: the ones currently offered.
* **Claimed rewards**: how many rewards were actually paid out.

:::note[Rewards pay out on their own]
There is nothing left to claim: the quest pays on the action that reaches the target, and the XP goes through the leveling system - level-up, reward roles and announcement included. The former `/quests claim` subcommand is gone.
:::

## Creating a quest

Click **New quest**. Kotbo first offers **pre-configured templates**:

| Template | Objective | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily Chatter** | Send 50 messages today | Daily |
| **Voice Chatter** | Spend 30 minutes in voice | Daily |
| **Reactor** | React to 20 messages | Daily |
| **Champion** | Win 3 games this week | Weekly |
| **Philanthropist** | Give 5 +rep this week | Weekly |
| **Creator** | Create 3 threads this week | Weekly |

The **Custom** option opens the full form:

  <div class="step-item"><div><h3>Type</h3><p>Send messages, Voice minutes, React to messages, Win a game, Earn coins, Give +rep, Create threads, Reply to messages.</p></div></div>

## Managing existing quests

Each quest card shows its **target**, its **coins** and **XP** rewards, and its number of **participations**.

Three actions are available:

* **Disable**: the quest stops being offered without being deleted.
* **Enable**: puts it back into circulation.
* **Delete**: removes it permanently.

:::tip[Disable rather than delete]
A disabled quest keeps its participation history. That is the right move for a seasonal quest you plan to bring back later.
:::

When no quest exists, Kotbo shows *No quest configured* and invites you to click **New quest**.
